⚖️ DUI STOP DEFENSE CARD

Four moments. Four lines. SAY ONCE · THEN SILENCE
💬

1. Questioning

“I’m not answering questions until I’ve spoken with my attorney.”
Berghuis v. Thompkins (2010) You must speak up — silence is not enough.
⏹️ After saying it once — stop. Don’t add “but…”. Re-engaging waives the right.
🧘

2. Field sobriety tests

“I respectfully decline the field sobriety tests.”
Pennsylvania v. Muniz (1990) Physical evidence ≠ testimony; tests are voluntary in most states.
🚫 No explanation. Just the line.
💨

3. Roadside breath test

“I’m declining the breath test.”
Birchfield v. North Dakota (2016) Roadside handheld ≠ station test. Refusal here is usually legal.
⚠️ Station test after arrest is different — implied consent laws apply.
🔍

4. Vehicle search

“I don’t consent to a search of the vehicle or myself.”
Schneckloth v. Bustamonte (1973) Police don’t have to tell you that you can refuse.
✋ Say it clearly, then stop. Do not argue. Keep hands visible.
⚡ FOUR LINES · ONE CALM VOICE
1. Not answering until lawyer. 2. Decline field tests. 3. Decline breath test. 4. No consent to search.
Then silence — it does the rest.
🚗💨 Send to someone who drives late.
