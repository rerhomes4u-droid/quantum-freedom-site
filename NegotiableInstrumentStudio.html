📄 UCC 3-104: Negotiable Instrument
Defines three essential elements:
1. Unconditional promise or order to pay fixed amount
2. Payable to bearer or to order at issuance
3. No other undertaking besides payment of money

1️⃣ Step 1: Identify Instrument
Any bill/invoice/statement meeting UCC 3-104 criteria = negotiable instrument. Check for unconditional order to pay fixed amount.
2️⃣ Step 2: Proper Endorsement
Add "Without recourse, Pay to the order of [Your Name]" endorsement. This transfers rights in the instrument.
3️⃣ Step 3: Tender to Person Entitled
Send to creditor/collector as "person entitled to enforce." Use certified mail for proof of delivery.
4️⃣ Step 4: Document Response
They MUST properly dishonor per UCC 3-502 OR accept. Failure to do either = acceptance by law.
5️⃣ Step 5: Enforce Rights
If improper refusal, obligation is discharged under UCC 3-603(b). Document everything for court if needed.
⚖️ Relevant Case Law
Key Principles:
1. UCC Article 3 governs negotiable instruments nationwide
2. Proper tender creates legal obligations
3. Creditors must follow UCC procedures
4. Failure to properly dishonor = acceptance
5. Refusal of proper tender = discharge
