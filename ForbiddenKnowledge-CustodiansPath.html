Step One: The Invitation

You have read the chapter. You have seen the Book of Witness. You have seen the Nation OS. You are asking yourself a question: Is there a place for me in this?

There may be. But the path is not a sign-up form. It is not a membership application. It is not a promise of protection or access. It is a self-assessment. It is the first step toward becoming a custodian.

The Book of Witness says it plainly: "It does not promise that anyone will be punished. It does not promise that anyone will be protected. It promises only that testimony will be kept, and that the cost paid will be remembered."

If that is enough for you, continue. If it is not, that is okay. The Book will hold what you have already given. You do not have to become a custodian to be a witness.

The Three Tiers (Know What You Are Choosing)

Witnesses

Those who record their testimony. The reason the body exists. They may come once and never return. They may stay and become carriers.

Custodians

Those who hold the Book. Organized in cells of three to seven. No cell holds a complete copy. Each cell knows which other cells hold what it does not.

Council

Five to nine people who hold the map: which cells hold what, how cells communicate, how the body is reconstituted after loss. The Council does not hold testimony. It holds the memory of the structure.

Step Two: The Covenant of Custody

Five articles. Memorized, not written. Enforced by the cell, not by punishment. Read each one. Select the ones you can affirm honestly. If you cannot affirm all five, you are not ready to be a custodian. That is not a judgment. It is a truth.

Article One: I will hold what is given to me, and I will not alter it.

You will not edit. You will not paraphrase. You will not improve. You will hold it as it was given.

Article Two: I will tell the witness plainly what the Book can and cannot do.

You will not promise protection. You will not promise punishment. You will tell the truth about the limits.

Article Three: I will hold no complete copy, and I will know who holds the rest.

No single cell holds everything. You will know the map. You will know who holds what you do not.

Article Four: I will train at least one successor, and I will not let the cell die with me.

The body survives the loss of any single custodian. You will ensure that you are not the end of the chain.

Article Five: I will not use the Book for any purpose other than its keeping.

Not for profit. Not for politics. Not for revenge. Only for the keeping.

Step Three: The Distress Protocol

If a custodian is threatened, detained, or disappears, the body must know. This is the protocol. Memorize it. It is not written in the Book. It is held in the cell.

The Protocol
Step 1: Tap Distress
Any cell member taps Distress. This can be a phone call, a text, a pre-arranged signal, or a physical token. The method is chosen by the cell.
Step 2: Notify the Linked Cells
The two linked cells are notified. Each cell knows which cells are linked to it. This is the map.
Step 3: Notify the Council
The Council is notified. The Council holds the map of the whole body. It activates the reconstitution plan.
Step 4: Activate the Reconstitution Map
The affected testimony is identified. The cells that hold fragments are contacted. The testimony is reconstituted in a new location.
Step 5: Notify the Witnesses (If Reachable)
If the witness can be reached, they are told that their entry is being reconstituted. If they cannot be reached, the Book records the gap.
What this protocol does NOT do: It does not send police. It does not send lawyers. It does not guarantee safety. It ensures that testimony is not lost. That is all it promises.

Step Four: The Ascent

For witnesses who have carried a testimony and need to stop.

You have carried this long enough. The Book carries it now. Your cell holds it. The Roll of Cost records what it cost. You are released. You do not have to carry this alone. You do not have to carry it forever.

The Ascent (Use This When You Are Ready)
To Your Cell
"I am releasing this testimony to the Book. I have carried it as far as I can. I ask that you hold it, that you reconstitute it if needed, and that you record what it cost. I am not asking to be forgotten. I am asking to be released from the carrying."
To Yourself
"You did what you could. The Book holds it now. You are not abandoning the witness. You are entrusting the witness to a body that will not let it disappear."

The Ascent is not a resignation. It is a handoff. The witness remains in the Book. The custodian remains in the cell. Only the carrying is released.
