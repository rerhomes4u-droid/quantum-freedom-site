THE MIRROR

An Interactive Breakdown of "We Are the Only Truly Responsible"

"El problema no es Abelardo. El problema somos nosotros."
— Rafael Pacheco, as read by Ramiro Ramirez

This app tests a single assertion: that we — the people, the voters, the consumers, the silent majority — are the only truly responsible ones for the state of our politics and our country. Not the politicians. Not the billionaires. Not the media. Us.

The assertion is not self-evidently true. It is not self-evidently false. It is a claim, and like every claim it must be broken into its parts, tested against the record, and marked where the record runs out. That is what this app does.

For each of the twelve components below, you will see the claim, the strongest case for it, and the strongest case against it. Then you must mark your own verdict. You cannot finish without taking a position on every piece. There is no "skip." There is no "neutral." There is only responsible, shared, not responsible, or record unclear.

Answer honestly. The mirror does not lie, but it only shows what you bring to it.

The mirror has done its work. It has not told you what to think. It has shown you what you think — and, more importantly, why you think it, and where the record supports you and where it does not.

If your verdicts skew toward "responsible," ask yourself what follows from that. Guilt without action is just another form of entertainment. If your verdicts skew toward "shared," ask yourself who the other shareholders are and whether you have named them specifically. If your verdicts skew toward "not responsible," ask yourself whether the record you relied on was complete — and whether you marked the gap honestly.

Responsibility is not a single point. It is a chain. Anyone who tells you the chain begins and ends in one place — whether that place is "the people" or "the elites" — is selling you something. The honest answer is usually: the chain is real, every link matters, and the link you can actually move is the one closest to your hands.

Now go do something with it.
