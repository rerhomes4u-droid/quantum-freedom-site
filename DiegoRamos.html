VA One-Time Close Construction Loans

For your qualified and active-duty military clients, building a new home may not be as intimidating as they once thought. A one-time close construction loan lets them roll the cost of the land, the build, and the permanent mortgage into one streamlined loan with a single closing.

Pro Tip: This program's standout benefit is no down payment required for eligible VA buyers. This means fewer moving parts, fewer surprises, and a smoother path to handing your military clients the keys to a brand-new home.

Seller-Funded Interest Rate Buydown

Instead of negotiating the price down, try negotiating a seller-funded interest rate buydown for one, two, or even three years! The seller still gets their full asking price, and the buyer enjoys easing into their new payments.
