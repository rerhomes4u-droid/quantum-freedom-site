UCC § 9-210

“Debtor may request an accounting” — and the secured party must comply within 14 days. Failure = bad faith, enforcement barred.

(a) “Accounting” means a record authenticated by a secured party indicating aggregate unpaid secured obligations as of a date not more than 35 days earlier.
(b) A debtor may request an accounting from a secured party.
(c) The secured party shall comply with a request within 14 days after receipt.
(e) If they fail: court may compel, award damages, limit enforcement, shift presumptions.

Reciprocal duty when enforcement, performance, or default issues arise. UCC § 1-304 (good faith) also compels accounting.

EQUITY · UCC SUPPORT

UCC § 9-607 & 9-608 — collection and enforcement must be commercially reasonable, proceeds correctly applied, surplus accounted.

Equitable accounting — when one party controls funds/obligations and info is uniquely held, equity demands an accounting. Trust, foreclosure, creditor-debtor cases.

“Secured party” and “debtor” are roles, not personal status. If someone demands payment, enforces possession, claims default, or controls collateral — accounting obligations trigger automatically.
