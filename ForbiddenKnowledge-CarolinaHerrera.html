▣ THE TOOLS SHE USED (AND YOU CAN USE)
THE MOTION TO COMPEL
Asks the court to order the police to produce the 911 audio and dashcam footage. Under Rule 37(a)(4), SCRCP, if the motion is granted, the court must require the non-producing party to pay the moving party's reasonable expenses, unless the opposition was substantially justified.
THE REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION
Rule 36(c), SCRCP limits requests to 20 per party. The rule states that if a party fails to respond within 30 days, the matter is admitted. If they respond with a false denial, they commit perjury. If they object without justification, the court can order an answer and award expenses.
THE NEGATIVE AVERMENTS
Puts the other side on notice. States what the record does not show. States what the other side has not alleged. Forces the other side to either produce evidence or admit the absence of evidence.
