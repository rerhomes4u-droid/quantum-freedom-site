The Priory of Sion is a Proven Forgery.

The idea that the Merovingian bloodline is the literal descendant of Jesus Christ and Mary Magdalene—the central claim of The Da Vinci Code and Holy Blood, Holy Grail—is built on a documented hoax.

The Truth: The Merovingian "bloodline" is real as a historical royal family, but the claim that they are the literal descendants of Jesus is a proven 20th-century forgery. The elite may *believe* the lie, but the lie was manufactured.
