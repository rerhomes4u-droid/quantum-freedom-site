📜 What is Project Esther? Released on October 7, 2024 — exactly one year after the Hamas attack — Project Esther is a 31-page policy blueprint created by the Heritage Foundation (the same think tank behind Project 2025). Named after the biblical queen who saved the Jews from persecution, the document claims to combat antisemitism but has been criticized for equating mainstream pro-Palestinian advocacy with support for terrorism.

Authors: Victoria Coates (former Trump deputy national security advisor), Robert Greenway, and Daniel Flesch. The plan explicitly aims to "dismantle the infrastructure that sustains the Hamas Support Network (HSN)" within 12–24 months under a "willing Administration."

⚠️ CRITICAL CONTEXT & CONTROVERSIES

Project Esther exclusively focuses on left-wing and pro-Palestinian activism while ignoring far-right antisemitism. The plan has been accused of conflating political speech with terrorism. In November 2025, the Zionist Organization of America and at least eight other Jewish groups withdrew from the Heritage task force after Heritage president Kevin Roberts defended Tucker Carlson's friendly interview with Nick Fuentes (white nationalist & antisemite). The project remains a cornerstone of the Heritage Foundation's influence on executive branch policy as of 2026.
