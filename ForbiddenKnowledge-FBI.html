The agent wrote that the case had been closed and that a prosecutor concurred with standard evidence-handling procedures. Under FBI policy, if evidence remains undisposed, the investigative file must remain open[citation:16].

By mid-2025, the DOJ sought to reconstruct the footage. Agents located a copy on a NiceVision digital video recorder. On May 21, 2025, an agent used a screen-capture tool to re-record it. The reconstruction left a 62-second gap[citation:16].
