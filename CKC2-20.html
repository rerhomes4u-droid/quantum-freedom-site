📄 The Complaint – Bite‑Sized Paragraphs Build Pressure

“Every allegation is a separate numbered paragraph. If you combine 4 points into one paragraph, they can deny the entire paragraph with one ‘Denied.’ By separating every point, they must answer each individually – ‘Affirmed,’ ‘Denied,’ or ‘I don’t know.’”
🔥 “The AMX complaint has ~156 numbered paragraphs. Each one is a tiny slice of truth. If they ‘Affirm,’ it’s proven. If they ‘Deny’ something true, that’s perjury. If they say ‘I don’t know,’ they lose credibility. Pressure is the point.”

Complaint sections:
- Jurisdiction & Venue (why this court has authority)
- Parties (plaintiff = ALL CAPS ens legis; defendant = the bank)
- General Factual Allegations (the story/definitions)
- Individual Counts (Causes of Action) (breach of contract, breach of fiduciary duty, fraud, etc.)
- Demand for Relief (what you want – damages, discharge, etc.)

⚖️ Jurisdiction – Naturalizing the ALL CAPS Name (8 USC §1101(a)(23))

“Naturalization means the conferring of nationality of a state upon a person after birth by any means whatsoever. I confer the nationality of the State of California upon the ALL CAPS strawman. That gives the federal court jurisdiction.”

Why this works: The plaintiff is the ALL CAPS ens legis (a legal fiction). By naturalizing it into the state where the court sits, you establish jurisdiction. The living man (Brandon Joe Williams) is not the plaintiff – he is the representative (attorney‑in‑fact).

📌 “Person Brandon Joe Williams, presented by man Brandon Joe Williams” – that’s the distinction. Two lines. No 9‑page manifesto. Simple.”

📬 Summons & Service of Process – 21‑Day Countdown

“You write the summons. The clerk signs it. You then hire a process server (non‑party) to deliver the summons and complaint to the defendant. The process server files an affidavit of service. Once served, the defendant has 21 days to respond (60 days for US government).”

If they fail to respond: You file a motion for default judgment. The court may award you everything you asked for. “That’s how easy it is – if they don’t answer.”

Process server recommendation: ABC Legal (~$80). You can serve via mail, but process servers are easier and create cleaner evidence.

🔪 Bite‑Sized Allegations – Examples from AMX Complaint

“Brandon J Williams had Cabbage loan number [X].

Each individual credit transaction produced an unconditional promise to pay.
A promissory note is an unconditional promise to pay.

🎯 “They can’t deny these. They are definitions from the UCC and the US Code. If they deny, they are denying the law itself. That’s a losing position.”

📋 Causes of Action – The “Counts” in the Complaint

Count 1 – Breach of Contract
They ignored your conditional acceptance/special endorsement. They failed to perform.

Count 2 – Breach of Fiduciary Duty
“AMX is not the lender – the ALL CAPS name is the lender. AMX is a fiduciary.”

Count 3 – Fraud
They never disclosed blank vs. special endorsement. Fraud by omission.

Count 4 – Peonage / Involuntary Servitude
Forcing you into a debt contract without adequate consideration or disclosure.

Count 5 – Enticement into Slavery
18 USC §1583 (civil remedy via 18 USC §1595).

Count 6 – Laundering of Monetary Instruments
Illegal transfer of your collateral securities.

⚡ Arbitration = TRASH – Kill It Before Filing

“Arbitration waves your right to a jury trial and Discovery. Discovery is where lawsuits are won (depositions, interrogatories, subpoenas). Arbitration is retired judges paid by the company. Send a Novation before litigation voiding all arbitration clauses due to fraud and lack of consideration.”
🧨 “If you get stuck in arbitration, you lose. I sent parcels to all parties canceling arbitration before I filed. Do not wait.”

💰 Demand for Relief – “250 Million or a Black Card”

“The demand for relief is what you want the court to award. I asked for:
- Full discharge of all loans
- Full refund of every payment ever made (in Federal Reserve notes)
- $250 million in damages
– OR – a Centurion Black Card with no limit, plus a limited power of attorney allowing AMX to endorse on my behalf.”

Why $250 million? “I just picked a number that felt comfortable. There’s no upper limit. The jury can award what they think is just. If they give me $100 million, that’s fine too.”

📜 Pro Se Litigants Get Leniency – But Not a Free Pass

“Pro se litigants have additional leniency from the court – about 10‑15% leeway on local rules. But you still need to learn the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and your local district rules. Law librarians can help.”

Local rules include: meet‑and‑confer requirements before motions, page limits, formatting. Messing these up can get you sanctioned – but as a pro se, you get a warning first typically.

🔍 Discovery – Where Lawsuits Are Won

“Discovery is when the judge issues you a badge, a gun, and a shovel. You can:
- Depose witnesses (video record them under penalty of perjury)
- Issue interrogatories (written questions under oath)
- Subpoena third parties for documents
- Force the other side to produce evidence
Discovery is the nuclear zone. Arbitration waves this. Never arbitrate.”
