Who Hashem Khader Abu Maria was

Hashem Khader Abu Maria was a Palestinian child rights advocate who worked with Defence for Children International - Palestine (DCI-Palestine). He dedicated his life to documenting and exposing violations against children in the occupied Palestinian territories.

On July 25, 2014, during Israel's Operation Protective Edge, Hashem was killed while peacefully protesting. The event was organized to honor the death of a fellow colleague and to discuss the role and risk of human rights defenders. He gave his life for the children of Palestine.

⚠️ The Circumstances

On July 25, 2014, Hashem Khader Abu Maria was killed while participating in a peaceful protest in the West Bank. The protest was organized to honor the death of a fellow colleague and to discuss the role and risk of human rights defenders. He was 34 years old. His death was part of a broader pattern of violence against Palestinian human rights defenders during the 2014 Gaza conflict.

📢

His Most Important Work

Advocating for the children of Palestine

📋 DCI-Palestine

Hashem worked with Defence for Children International - Palestine, a non-governmental organization dedicated to promoting and protecting the rights of Palestinian children. DCI-Palestine documents violations of children's rights, provides legal aid, and advocates for justice.

Organization

📝 Documenting Violations

His work involved documenting violations against children in the occupied Palestinian territories, including arbitrary detention, ill-treatment, and the prosecution of children in military courts. He was committed to exposing these abuses to the world.

Documentation

⚖️ Legal Advocacy

Hashem was involved in legal advocacy for Palestinian children, working to ensure that their rights were protected under international law. He believed that every child deserved a fair hearing and humane treatment.

Legal Aid

🕊️ Peaceful Protest

Hashem participated in peaceful protests to raise awareness about the plight of Palestinian children. On the day of his death, he was attending a peaceful gathering to honor a fellow colleague and discuss the risks faced by human rights defenders.

Peaceful Action

🌍 International Awareness

His work contributed to international awareness of the treatment of Palestinian children in the occupied territories. DCI-Palestine reports have been cited by the United Nations and human rights organizations worldwide.

Global Impact

💔 Sacrifice for Justice

Hashem gave his life for the cause he believed in — the protection of children. His death serves as a reminder of the dangers faced by human rights defenders and the importance of their work.

Martyrdom

⚠️ Critical Note

Hashem was killed during a peaceful protest, not during combat. He was a civilian human rights defender who dedicated his life to protecting children. His death highlights the dangers faced by those who speak out against violations of children's rights.
