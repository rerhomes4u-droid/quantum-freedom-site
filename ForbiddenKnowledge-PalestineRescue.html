01The Fund Structure

The Palestine Rescue Fund sits inside the Amnesty Coalition for Unity PMA. It is a designated purpose within the existing trust structure. It has three pools. Each pool has its own accounting, its own reporting, and its own protocol.

Pool 1 — Extraction

Purpose: Transport, visas, legal fees, document recovery, safe passage for individuals and families with family ties to Colombia or with refugee status.

Unit: One extraction sponsorship = one person's transport + first month of sanctuary.

Target: 50 extractions by the end of the first operational quarter.

Pool 2 — Sanctuary

Purpose: Housing, food, medical care, legal support, and integration for the first 12 months in Colombia.

Unit: One sanctuary membership = one PMA member or family hosting one person or family for a defined period.

Target: 20 host commitments before the first arrival.

Pool 3 — Witness

Purpose: Documentation, legal advocacy, and public record of every person helped. Every dollar tracked. Every person recorded (anonymized).

Unit: The public ledger. Updated weekly. Auditable by any member.

Target: 100% of funds accounted for, 100% of the time.

02The Campaign Mechanics

The record is clear: open-ended campaigns lose urgency. Specific goals with deadlines work. Peer-to-peer fundraising multiplies reach. The Rescue Fund uses four mechanisms.

Extraction Sponsorship — The Unit

One sponsorship funds one person's extraction and first month. The sponsor sees the unit cost. The sponsor sees the target. The sponsor sees the deadline. The sponsor sees the number funded. The sponsor does not see the person's name.

UNIT COST: $2,400
INCLUDES: Transport, visa, legal fee, first month sanctuary
TARGET: 50 units by Q2
FUNDED: 0 / 50
Witness Network — Peer-to-Peer

PMA members commit to raising a specific amount through their own networks. Each member has a page. Each page has a target. Each page is tracked on the public ledger. The member keeps 100% of what they raise for the fund. The fund keeps 0% of the member's raise. The member's name is public. The rescued person's name is never public.

Sanctuary Membership — In-Kind

A PMA member pledges to host one person or family for a defined period. The pledge is documented. The host is vetted. The host is registered with the Colombian attorney. The host receives a monthly stipend from the fund for food and utilities. The host's identity is known to the fund. The host's identity is not public.

Legacy Giving — The Trust

Members can designate a portion of their estate to the Rescue Fund through the existing trust structure. The designation is documented. The fund is the beneficiary. The trust is the vehicle. The public ledger records the designation without the amount.

03The Code Word System

Every member of the PMA who is in a danger zone has a code word. The code word is ordinary. It is unique. It is known only to the member and the response team. When the member sends the code word—by text, by email, by a call to a monitored number—the response protocol activates.

05The Legal Architecture

The Rescue Fund operates inside the PMA. The PMA operates in the private domain. The fund is not a public charity soliciting the general public. It is a private fund within a private association, governed by private agreements between members.

The PMA

The Amnesty Coalition for Unity PMA is a voluntary association of individuals who share a common purpose. Members join by application. Members agree to the bylaws. Members are bound by private dispute resolution. The PMA operates in the private domain, outside the reach of public regulatory bodies that govern public charities.

The Trust

The Rescue Fund sits inside the existing trust structure. The trustees are fiduciaries. The trust deed defines the purpose. The accounting is separate. The public ledger is the trust's reporting mechanism. The trust is the legal owner of the funds. The PMA is the operator.

The Colombian Attorney

The Colombian attorney on retainer handles every visa application, every legal question, every interaction with Colombian immigration. The fund does not smuggle. The fund does not break laws. The fund pays for lawful entry, lawful residence, and lawful integration.

The Sanctuary Hosts

Every host is a PMA member. Every host is vetted. Every host is registered with the Colombian attorney. Every host signs a hosting agreement. Every host receives a stipend. Every host's identity is known to the fund and unknown to the public.

06What This Dashboard Is Not

This is not the public donation page. That page is separate, and it is built for people who want to sponsor an extraction or join the Witness Network. It has the unit cost. It has the target. It has the deadline. It has the public ledger. It does not have the code words, the routes, the hosts, or the attorneys.

This is not a promise that the fund will succeed. It is the architecture. The success depends on the members who run it, the donors who fund it, and the hosts who open their homes.

This is not a call to break any law. It is a call to use the law. The PMA is lawful. The trust is lawful. The visas are lawful. The sanctuary is lawful. The rescue is lawful. The nation follows Colombian immigration law to the letter.
