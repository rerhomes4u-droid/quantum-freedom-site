Gustavo Petro

The Interview After the Call · Al Jazeera, January 2026

Context. Gustavo Petro is the President of Colombia. He is the first left-wing president in the country's history [citation:1][citation:14]. He was a member of the M-19 guerrilla movement, demobilized in 1990, and spent decades in politics before winning the presidency in 2022 [citation:6][citation:7]. This transcript is from an interview with Al Jazeera after his phone call with Donald Trump. The quotes are his own words, translated from Spanish.

On Sovereignty

Record · Direct Quote
"The principle of national sovereignty has been challenged in Latin America. And Latin America was founded on that principle, just like the United States—against kings, against emperors. And that implies respecting that principle. If not, everything falls apart."
Petro is responding to the US intervention in Venezuela and the capture of Nicolás Maduro.
Record · Direct Quote
"The Monroe Doctrine has been revived in norm. The belief in a right over Latin America that does not exist."
Petro is naming the doctrine that the United States has used to justify intervention in the Western Hemisphere for two centuries.
Record · Direct Quote
"If the principle of national sovereignty is destroyed—the principle on which international law, the United Nations, etc., are based—then barbarism would not only be in Latin America. Barbarism would expand throughout the world."
Petro is warning that the violation of sovereignty in one place sets a precedent for everywhere.

On War

Record · Direct Quote
"A third world war is coming. I don't know at what moment, but everything is heading there if this continues."
Petro is responding to a question about the state of multilateralism.
Record · Direct Quote
"You know what a world war means in times of climate change. It is like combining all the conditions of the perfect storm against the life of humanity itself, including the society of the United States."
Petro is connecting war to the climate crisis.

On the Drug War

Record · Direct Quote
"The traditional Colombian political power was the best ally of drug trafficking in Colombia. What they wanted was to win elections here, or if not, launch violence, break relations between the United States and Colombia—from which only drug trafficking would benefit."
Petro is describing the political dynamic he says he inherited.
Record · Direct Quote
"I have been careful—despite the insults, the threats—to maintain cooperation on drug trafficking between Colombia and the United States. I know what it means if that breaks. In Colombia, it means the narcos return to political power. They have been there, and every time they have been there, they have unleashed a genocide in Colombia. I cannot be that irresponsible."
Petro is explaining why he continued cooperating with the US despite Trump's insults.
Record · Direct Quote
"It is a scissors strategy. You win over the peasant, you make peace with the peasant, he removes the coca leaf crops, and you attack where the capital is."
Petro is describing his drug policy: voluntary substitution, not forced eradication.

On the Rules of the World

Record · Direct Quote
"The rules of the world have to be obligatory. They can be improved over time, and the United States can help improve them. But if what is imposed is breaking them by brute force, then brute force comes. And brute force first is barbarism."
Petro is responding to a question about multilateralism.
Record · Direct Quote
"Why didn't they take Netanyahu, but they took Maduro? Anyone asks themselves why. Because Maduro is Latin American. Because Mr. Netanyahu is a financial partner of the international world."
Petro is comparing the ICC arrest warrant for Netanyahu with the US capture of Maduro.
