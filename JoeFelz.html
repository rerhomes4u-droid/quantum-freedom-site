Who Joe Felz was

Joe Felz was an entrepreneur, real estate investor, off-grid lifestyle advocate, and truth seeker who dedicated his final months to uncovering and sharing suppressed knowledge with the world. He was the founder of Felz Investment Group (2011), and later founded Felz Books, Felz Energy, and Felz Media Group.

At the time of his death, Joe was actively working to digitize rare and ancient Bibles, Masonic texts, and books on free energy, planning to make them freely available to the public. He had amassed over 1.4 million followers across social media platforms.
