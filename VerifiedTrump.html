📌 People v. Trump (NY) GUILTY · 34 FELONIES
New York Supreme Court | Indictment 2023 | Verdict May 2024

Charges: 34 counts falsifying business records to conceal hush money payments.

Outcome: Convicted on all counts. Sentence: unconditional discharge (no jail, but conviction stands). Appeal pending as of 2026.

✅ Court finding: Guilty — first felony conviction of a former U.S. president.
⚖️ Carroll v. Trump (SDNY) LIABLE · $88.3M
1:20-cv-07311 | Jury verdicts 2023 & 2024

Finding: Civil liability for sexual abuse (battery) and defamation. Incident occurred in 1996.

Damages: $5M (battery/defamation) + $83.3M (post-verdict defamation) = $88.3 million.

📜 Jury determined Trump forcibly subjected Carroll to sexual contact. Defeated truth defense.
🧾 NY v. Trump (Fraud) PENALTY VOIDED ON APPEAL
New York AG civil action | Feb 2024 ruling | Aug 2025 appeal

Trial finding: Trump habitually inflated net worth on financial statements (fraud).

Original penalty: $355M + interest. Appeals court threw out monetary penalty as excessive; left corporate bans in place. NY AG appealing to higher court.

⚠️ Factual finding of persistent fraud remains — only the fine was vacated on constitutional grounds.
🛡️ Federal criminal cases DISMISSED / DROPPED

U.S. v. Trump (Jan 6 / election case): Charges dropped Nov 2024 (DOJ policy after election victory).

U.S. v. Trump (classified documents, FL): Dismissed July 2024. Judge Cannon ruled Special Counsel Smith's appointment unconstitutional — case dismissed without prejudice to potential refiling.

⚖️ Dismissal was procedural (Appointments Clause) — not a ruling on innocence/guilt of the underlying allegations.
