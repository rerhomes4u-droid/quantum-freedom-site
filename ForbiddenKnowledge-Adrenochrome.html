That is the gap. Not "adrenochrome is real." The gap is: the official narrative has no satisfying answer to the real trafficking it debunks alongside the fake drug.

Claim: Adrenochrome has anti-aging properties

Disputed → Record does not support

Name the specific: That adrenochrome extends lifespan or reverses aging.

Corroboration: No peer-reviewed study supports this. The compound has no established medical use. Early mid-20th-century psychiatric experimentation with adrenochrome (Abram Hoffer, 1950s) explored it as a possible psychotomimetic — not as an anti-aging agent — and those studies were not replicated.

Gap: None on this claim. The literature is thin but consistent: no anti-aging effect.

Refuse to overclaim: The record does not support anti-aging claims. It supports "unremarkable oxidized epinephrine."
