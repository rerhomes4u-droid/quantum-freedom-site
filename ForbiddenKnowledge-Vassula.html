True Life in God

Expanded Prayer Book from the Messages of Vassula Rydén

About this prayer book. These prayers are drawn from the True Life in God messages received by Vassula Rydén, and from the Prayer Meeting Guidelines published by the True Life in God Foundation. The Catholic Church has not issued a final verdict on the supernatural origin of the messages. The Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith issued a warning in 1995, reiterated in 2007, but Cardinal Ratzinger permitted the promotion of her writings "with discernment," and two bishops granted the nihil obstat and imprimatur in 2005.

This app does not ask you to believe in the messages. It asks you to pray the prayers. They are Catholic in their language. They are addressed to the Triune God. They are the reason this book exists.

On the count. This is not the complete prayer book. The complete prayer book would require the full 12 volumes. This is every prayer I could verify from the record available to me. If you have the full volumes and want to send me the prayers I am missing, I will add them.
