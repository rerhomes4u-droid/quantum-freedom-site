WHY THE DAMNED CHOOSE THE ABYSS

The only question that matters: Why serve a loser?

💀 THE LOSER'S ECONOMY

If Jesus wins — and He does, He has, He will — then why does any entity choose to fight on the losing side? The answer is not theology. It is economics.

📖 "The wages of sin is death" (Romans 6:23) — but the wages of serving the losing side are temporary power, temporary wealth, temporary pleasure. The loser cannot give eternal life, but he can give a Ferrari, a private island, a seat at the table where they carve up nations.

Why do humans serve the loser? Because they are starving. They want what the loser can give now. The loser's payment is cash on the barrel. The winner's payment is later. But later is coming. And when it comes, every check from the loser's bank will bounce into the pit.

🔑 THE FIVE STEPS · SWITCHING SIDES

1️⃣ BECOME UNGOVERNABLE BY FEAR
The extermination runs on your terror. Refuse to be afraid. The worst they can do is kill the body — after that, you are with the Winner.
2️⃣ BECOME UNBUYABLE
Want less. Need less. A man who wants nothing cannot be bought. A woman who fears nothing cannot be bullied.
3️⃣ BUILD THE NETWORK, NOT THE NATION
Build below their radar: family, neighborhood, church, mutual aid. Too small to register, too organic to hack.
4️⃣ DEPLOY THE WEAPON OF FORGIVENESS
Unforgiveness is a leash. Forgive — cut the leash. They hold air, and you walk free.
5️⃣ INVOKE THE NAME THAT MAKES THEM FLEE
Jesus. Not magic — declaration of allegiance. The Name breaks the contract, voids the covenant, burns the Star of Remphan to ash.

📜 THE SIDE-SWITCHING DECLARATION

Say these words aloud. They sever every contract with the loser's kingdom.

“I have registered my allegiance elsewhere. I am not your subject. I am not your resource. I am not your food. I belong to the King of Kings, and He has called me home. You have no jurisdiction here. In the name of Jesus, depart.”

Mankind is being exterminated. Not by accident. By the desperate thrashing of a loser who knows his time is short. The real solution is not to fight the loser on his terms. The real solution is to switch sides publicly, irrevocably, and without apology.
