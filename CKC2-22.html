🚔 The Three Assumptions – Duty, Breach, Damages

Every litigation situation (including a traffic stop) involves three elements: DUTY · BREACH · DAMAGES
The officer makes three assumptions that create his perceived duty:
🔥 Assumption 1: That you are “driving” (49 CFR §390.5 – “driver means any person who operates any commercial motor vehicle”).
🔥 Assumption 2: That you are inside a “motor vehicle” (defined as used for transporting persons/property for compensation).
🔥 Assumption 3: That the transaction is taking place inside a business listed in Dun & Bradstreet called “State of California.”

“If you blow out these three pillars, the officer has no duty. No duty = no breach. No breach = no damages. You can then countersue under 42 USC §1983 for deprivation of rights.”

📄 Motion to Dismiss – The First Shiny Object

“A motion to dismiss is filed under Rule 12(b)(6) – failure to state a claim upon which relief can be granted. The defense will ALWAYS file one, even if your case is air‑tight. It’s their job: redirect your attention away from the door.”
🎯 “The bar card’s entire job is to distract you with shiny objects – dancing girls, fountains of whiskey, mirages. The motion to dismiss is their first distraction. If you ignore it or don’t oppose it, they win by default.”

How to oppose: Show that your complaint states a duty (the bank’s obligation), a breach (failure to honor endorsements), and damages (loss of value, stress, financial harm). If those three are present, the motion to dismiss should be denied.

⚖️ Motion for Summary Judgment – Filed Before Discovery = Fear

“Summary judgment is a motion that says: ‘There are no genuine disputes of material fact, so I win as a matter of law.’ If they file it BEFORE Discovery, they are AFRAID of Discovery.”
💣 “The Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (Rule 56) allow summary judgment only after Discovery or if no facts are in dispute. If they file early, you oppose with: ‘There are material facts in dispute – specifically, the nature of the underlying negotiable instruments, which Discovery would uncover.’ The court will deny summary judgment and order Discovery.”

Why they fear Discovery: “Discovery gives you a badge, a gun, and a shovel. You can depose their witnesses, subpoena their records, and force them to answer interrogatories under penalty of perjury. They don’t want that.”

🔍 Discovery – Issued a Badge, Gun & Shovel

“Discovery is when the court grants you the power to investigate. You become a detective. You can:”

Depositions: Question witnesses on camera under penalty of perjury. If they lie, they go to prison.

Interrogatories: Written questions under oath.

Subpoenas: Force third parties (banks, Fed, etc.) to produce documents.

Requests for Production: Force the opposing party to hand over evidence.

🎯 “Once you get into Discovery, the bar cards become your best friends. They’ll want to settle. Because they know you’ll find the truth. And the truth destroys them.”

🧠 “Infant or Person of Unsound Mind” – Why Bar Cards Make You a Ward

“Client (Corpus Juris Secundum): ‘One who applies to a lawyer.’ Clients are also called wards of the court. Wards of the court are defined as infants and persons of unsound mind.”
⚠️ “When you hire an attorney‑at‑law (bar card), you are declaring to the court that you cannot speak for yourself. You are an infant. You are of unsound mind. You become property of the court – a res of a trust. The attorney administers the assets of the court (you).”

Solution: Act as an attorney‑in‑fact (non‑bar, under a power of attorney). Represent the ALL CAPS strawman, not yourself. Do not become a “client.”

📜 “License to Practice Law” – The Definition Breakthrough

“License (Black’s Law): ‘Permission to do something that would otherwise be not allowable.’
Practicing law = administering the assets of the court (the wards).
A bar card is a membership card to a private association. It is not a license. It is permission to administer the court’s property – which are infants and persons of unsound mind.”

🔄 Novation – The Silent Contract Amendment

“Novation means a change in a contract. When you send new terms and conditions to a counterparty, if they do not respond, their silence is acquiescence. The contract is now amended under the new terms.”
🎯 “I send Novations constantly. They never respond. So now the contract is whatever I say it is. That’s how I changed all my endorsements from blank to special. They never objected – so they agreed.”

📜 Pro Se Leniency – But Not a License to Be Sloppy

“Pro se litigants are held to less stringent standards than lawyers – but not a free pass. You still need to follow the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and your district’s local rules.”

Key local rule example (C.D. Cal. 7-3): requires a meet and confer before filing motions – preferably in person. If the defense violates this, you can move to strike their motion.

⚾ Baseball Is More Complicated Than Law – It’s Just Novelty

“People say law is too complicated. But baseball has the infield fly rule, the strike zone, balks, the shift – it’s madness. You learned baseball because you were exposed to it. Law is the same – you just haven’t been exposed. Learn the vocabulary. It’s not harder.”
🔥 “The only thing that makes law feel harder is the EMOTIONAL WEIGHT. You’re scared because your freedom or money is on the line. Once you realize the other side are just infants throwing tantrums, the fear goes away.”
