1 Who Was Isaac Kappy?

Actor known for small roles in Thor, Terminator Salvation, Breaking Bad (S2E1) and others.

In July 2018 at San Diego Comic-Con, he publicly accused several high-profile Hollywood figures (including Seth Green, Tom Hanks, Steven Spielberg and others) of being involved in pedophilia networks.

He claimed to be feeding information to the Trump administration via a "White House contact" and was a vocal early supporter of QAnon.

After his accusations, he was largely blacklisted from Hollywood roles.
