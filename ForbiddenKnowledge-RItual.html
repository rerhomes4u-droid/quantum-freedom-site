Spirit Cooking is a 1996 performance piece. Abramovic painted instructions on a wall with pig's blood. The "recipes" include "fresh breast milk with fresh sperm milk" and "fresh morning urine" [citation:24]. The image of Podesta with a pentagram on his hand was photoshopped. The original image was from a UN campaign for ocean protection. The fish and the number 14 were from the campaign logo .
