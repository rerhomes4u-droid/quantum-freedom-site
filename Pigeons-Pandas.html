01 The Baby Pigeon: The Joke That Became a Movement

The Claim

Record: Satirical parody, created 2017 Suppressed: The biodrone technology is real

The movement has rallies, merchandise, and a book. It mocks QAnon and other conspiracy theories by taking their logic to an absurd extreme [citation:1].

The system is powered by a miniature solar battery. The developers claim successful test flights .

02 The Pandas: The Theory and the Robot Video

The Claim

Record: TikTok meme, 6.2M views Suppressed: The robot pandas were real
The Record

The "pandas aren't real" theory is a TikTok meme. The hashtag #pandasnotreal has over 6.2 million views. The claim: pandas are men in costumes or painted bears. The reasons: they are too cute, their reproduction is absurdly difficult, and they seem "drugged" [citation:13].

The theory is not new. Reddit threads from 2015 discuss it. One post: "I am almost sure that pandas are just an extremely elaborate hoax" [citation:13].

The theory is false as a general claim. Pandas are real animals. They are a national treasure in China. They live in the wild. They are not men in costumes.

The video exists. One hundred "pandas" appeared on stage. They were machines. The performance was broadcast nationally. The engineer explained the process: 3D molding, heat dissipation holes, cooling fans, two months of adjustments [citation:2].
