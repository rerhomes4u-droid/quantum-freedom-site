THE ASCENT

Trauma is a burden. It can crush you. It can lead to a sad life. It can lead to the early termination of it by suicide—a stupid mistake.

Or it can be your Tackling Fuel.

▣ THE CHOICE

Many people are consumed by their trauma. They let it dictate their worth, their relationships, and their future. They become victims of their own history.

But there is another path. You can take the exact same pain that was meant to destroy you and use it as the fuel to get solutions out to the world. You can turn it into a gift.

The ultimate goal is to forgive the person who harmed you.

We know it is super hard. We know it might take years. We know you might not be able to completely do it yet. That is why this app exists. To help free you—not the person who harmed you.

▣ THE MISSION

Your tackling fuel was used to defend the innocent. To defend those who can't defend themselves. They can't defend themselves because they lack the Forbidden Knowledge contained in this archive.

By releasing the weight of the trauma, you free yourself up to distribute this knowledge. You free yourself up to save mankind.

Forgiveness is not saying what they did was okay. Forgiveness is refusing to let them drive your car any longer.

▣ THE PROTOCOL

Step 1: Identify the Weight

Write down the name of the person, the institution, or the event that harmed you. Write down what they did. Do not censor yourself. Do not soften it. Acknowledge the weight of the burden.

Step 2: The Pardon (For Your Sake)

Write a statement of forgiveness. Not because they deserve it. But because you have a mission to complete and you cannot carry this dead weight while you do it. Write the words you need to say to let it go.
