01 The Teeth: Palm Beach Pete

The Claim

Record: Pete denies it, Grok fact-check confirms separate person Suppressed: The teeth are a match
The Record

Pete has repeatedly denied it. "I'm not Jeffrey Epstein. He's dead. I'm just Palm Beach Pete going to play tennis and have lunch" [citation:2].

Pete's denial is not accepted by the people who believe it. They see the denial as part of the cover-up. The man who looks like Epstein says he is not Epstein. That is exactly what Epstein would say.

Record: FBI file contains the claim, unverified Suppressed: The staging proves the cover-up

The real body was moved in a black vehicle "without anyone noticing" [citation:3].

The report notes the summary is not the official death file and "cannot be fully verified" [citation:3].

The Suppressed

The staging does not prove he is alive. It proves they wanted the media to follow the wrong vehicle. Why? What were they moving? The record does not say.

GAP: The fake body is in an FBI file, but the file itself says it cannot be fully verified. The official death investigation did not adopt it. The record contains the claim. It does not contain confirmation.

03 The Missing Minute: The Master Tape Was Destroyed

The Claim

The surveillance video has a gap. The gap was caused by a nightly reset. Nothing to see here.

Record: Master tape destroyed, explanation untested Suppressed: They destroyed the evidence
The Record

The FBI destroyed its master copy of the surveillance video. An evidence item labeled 1B60 was authorized for destruction in June 2024, described as "no longer pertinent" [citation:12].

When the DOJ needed it in 2025, they reconstructed it from a screen recording of a DVR. The 62-second gap happened because the screen capture tool couldn't capture that segment [citation:12].

The "nightly reset" explanation was a theory the FBI's own video specialist "was unable to test." The FBI section chief wrote: "The Video Specialist theorized... The Video Specialist was unable to test the accuracy of his theory" [citation:12].

Experts told CBS News the time-delay theory was implausible. None of the security system specialists CBS spoke with had heard of a system with that issue [citation:12].

The Suppressed

The suppressed claim: They destroyed the master tape. The copy has a hole. The explanation is unverified. The official story is that nothing happened in that minute. The record is that the tape that could prove it was destroyed.

The gap is not "what happened in the minute." The gap is "why was the master tape destroyed in the first place."

GAP: The master tape is gone. The copy has a 62-second hole. The official explanation is a theory the FBI itself could not test. That is the gap.

04 The Orange Figure: He Was Not Alone

The Claim

An orange figure was seen moving toward Epstein's tier at 10:39 PM. The official story says he was alone. The footage says otherwise.

Record: Dispute between FBI and DOJ, official report adopted officer explanation Suppressed: It was a prisoner. He was not alone.
The Record

The FBI files contain a dispute over an "orange shadow" moving up the stairs toward Epstein's tier at 10:39 PM on August 9, 2019 [citation:11].

The FBI's analysis log said it "could possibly be an inmate being escorted." The DOJ Inspector General's office said it was "possibly a correctional officer carrying inmate sheets or bedding." The final official report adopted the officer explanation [citation:11].

An independent video analyst said the movement was more consistent with a prisoner in orange. Prison staff said moving a prisoner at 10:30 PM was "very unusual" [citation:11].

The Suppressed

The suppressed claim: The official story is that Epstein was alone in his tier. The footage shows a figure in orange walking toward his cell. The FBI's own analyst said it could be a prisoner. The final report chose the officer explanation. Why?

The contradiction is in the record. The official claim of "alone" and the orange figure cannot both be true.

GAP: The FBI and DOJ disagreed on what the orange figure was. The final report adopted one explanation. The independent analyst disagreed. The record contains the contradiction.
