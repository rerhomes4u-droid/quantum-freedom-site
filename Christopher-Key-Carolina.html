PART 1: THE ARSENAL OF TRUTH

⚠️ WARNING: These strategies are designed to expose the system. They are not a substitute for a licensed attorney, but they are the tools that attorneys will never teach you.

1. NEVER CONSENT TO JURISDICTION

Do not simply accept that a court has the right to tell you what to do. You must always be ready to challenge "Subject Matter Jurisdiction."

How to do it: In any filing, state: "The Defendant appears specially, for the sole purpose of challenging subject matter jurisdiction, and does not waive any rights by so appearing."

2. THE UNREBUTTED AFFIDAVIT

An affidavit is a legal document. If you serve a sworn affidavit on an opposing party and they fail to contest it point-by-point, it stands as truth in law.

How to do it: Draft a 200-point affidavit. Serve it on them. If they say "I deny all," they are in violation of the rules of evidence. If they refuse to respond, you have won.

3. PRIMA FACIE EVIDENCE

Prima facie evidence is evidence that establishes a fact unless rebutted. Once you establish your facts, the burden shifts to them to prove otherwise.

How to do it: Use your affidavit to establish the facts of your case. Make them respond to the facts, or the facts are admitted.

4. THE NEGATIVE AVERMENT STRATEGY

A negative averment is a denial of an allegation. When you assert a negative averment, the burden of proof shifts to the opposing party to prove the positive.

How to do it: State, "I deny the jurisdiction of this court because it has failed to prove its subject matter jurisdiction." The burden is now on the court to prove it has jurisdiction.

5. THE ABATEMENT STRATEGY

Abatement means to suspend or stop legal proceedings. If the State Court is violating your due process rights, you can file a Notice of Abatement to stop the proceedings and move the case to Federal Court.

How to do it: File a Notice of Abatement in the State Court, citing the Supremacy Clause. Then file a Motion to Remove to Federal Court.

6. THE UCC-1 FINANCING STATEMENT

Did you know you can file a UCC-1 to protect your fundamental rights? In the case of Carolina, we filed a UCC-1 against SCDSS to secure the return of her son, E.B.H. This is a powerful "deb" collection tool.
