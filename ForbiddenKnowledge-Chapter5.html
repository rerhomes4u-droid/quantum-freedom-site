01 The PREP Act: Immunity, Documented

The Claim

A law passed in 2005 gave drug companies complete immunity from liability for "covered countermeasures" during public health emergencies. They gave themselves immunity before they released the product.

Record: Enacted Dec 30, 2005 Suppressed: The immunity was the architecture
The Record

The PREP Act was enacted December 30, 2005, as Public Law 109-148, Division C, Section 2 [citation:1][citation:16]. It amended the Public Health Service Act, adding Section 319F-3 (liability immunity) and Section 319F-4 (compensation program) [citation:1].

The immunity is broad. Covered persons "shall be immune from suit and liability under Federal and State law with respect to all claims for loss caused by, arising out of, relating to, or resulting from the administration to or the use by an individual of a covered countermeasure" [citation:18].

The only exception is "willful misconduct" that proximately causes serious physical injury or death [citation:18].

The COVID-19 declaration was issued and amended repeatedly. By 2021, amendments expanded "qualified persons" to include pharmacists, pharmacy interns, technicians, midwives, paramedics, EMTs, and students, and preempted state laws to the contrary [citation:2].

The Suppressed

The chapter's claim that the Act was "shoved through at 11:20 PM on a Saturday night" is not verified in the record I can reach. The Act was part of a Defense Appropriations bill. The specific time and procedural maneuvering are not in the search results. Label as unverified.

What is verified: The immunity structure existed before the COVID-19 pandemic. It was activated, amended, and expanded when COVID-19 arrived. The architecture was in place.

The suppressed claim: They did not need to "plan" the COVID-19 response in secret. They had already built the legal architecture that would make accountability impossible. They just had to turn the key.

  • Congressional Research Service: The PREP Act and COVID-19 congress.gov Official analysis of the amendments that expanded the pool of "qualified persons" and preempted state law.
GAP: The procedural claim ("11:20 PM Saturday") is unverified. The statutory claim (immunity was enacted in 2005, activated for COVID-19) is verified.

02 The Nuremberg Code: Consent, Documented

The Claim

The Nuremberg Code says voluntary consent is absolutely essential, without force, fraud, deceit, duress, or coercion. The mandates violated it.

Record: Code text confirms consent requirement Gap: No court ruling on mandate violation
The Record

The Nuremberg Code's first principle: "The voluntary consent of the human subject is absolutely essential. This means that the person involved should have legal capacity to give consent; should be so situated as to be able to exercise free power of choice, without the intervention of any element of force, fraud, deceit, duress, over-reaching, or other ulterior form of constraint or coercion" [citation:4].

The Code exists. It was established after the Nazi doctors' trial. The text is public. It is not disputed.

The mandates existed. Vaccination was required for employment, travel, and in some jurisdictions, school attendance. Penalties included termination, exclusion, and fines.

The Suppressed

The suppressed claim: The mandates were coercion. The penalties were duress. The "safe and effective" messaging was fraud. Therefore, the Nuremberg Code was violated.

The record does not contain a court ruling on this question. The PREP Act immunity prevented the question from reaching a courtroom. No judge has ruled on whether the mandates violated the Nuremberg Code. The question is not "did the courts rule against the mandates?" The question is "were the courts allowed to rule at all?"

The gap is procedural, not factual. The Code exists. The mandates existed. The immunity prevented the collision from being adjudicated.

03 The CICP: The Black Hole, Documented

The Claim

The US government has paid out over $4.4 billion in vaccine injury compensation, yet Pfizer and Moderna have paid $0.

Record: NVICP paid $4B+, CICP paid almost nothing Suppressed: COVID injuries go to CICP, not NVICP
The Record

The NVICP was established in 1986. It has paid out over $4 billion since its inception [citation:5][citation:11]. It covers 15 childhood vaccines and the seasonal flu vaccine [citation:11].

But COVID-19 vaccine injuries are not handled by NVICP. They go to the Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program (CICP), a separate program created by the PREP Act [citation:1].

The CICP has been described as a "legal black hole." As of 2026 data: 14,146 claims filed. Only 62 awarded compensation [citation:20]. A February 2025 data release showed 26 paid out of 13,659 claims — less than 0.2% [citation:27].

The CICP has no judge, no court, no right of appeal to a higher court. A nameless HHS administrator reviews claims and decides compensation with no public oversight [citation:20].

The Suppressed

The chapter's $4.4 billion figure is roughly correct for NVICP, but it is misleading in context. The NVICP has been running since 1988. The $4 billion is spread over three decades. The COVID-19 vaccines were not covered by NVICP. They were covered by CICP, which has paid out almost nothing.

The suppressed claim: The government created a compensation program for COVID-19 injuries that is designed to fail. The CICP is not a remedy. It is a procedural wall.

Pfizer and Moderna have paid $0 in CICP awards because the CICP is government-funded. They have paid $0 in court because the PREP Act prevented them from being in court. Both facts are true.

  • mctlaw: COVID-19 Vaccine Injury Compensation — The CICP Black Hole mctlaw.com Law firm analysis. 14,146 claims, 62 paid. "No transparency, no court, no judge, no right of appeal."
  • mctlaw: February 2025 Data Release mctlaw.com 26 paid out of 13,659 claims. Average payout (excluding outliers) $4,000. Most myocarditis claims $1,000–$5,500.
GAP: The $4.4 billion figure is real for NVICP. It is not COVID-19 compensation. COVID-19 injuries go to CICP, which has paid almost nothing. The chapter conflates two programs. The record shows the CICP is the black hole.
