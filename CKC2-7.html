🔢 Endorsement Codes – The Tan Zone (DNFR / END# / EXP)

On the DS-11, upper right tan section: "DNFR", "END#", "EXP" – these are NOT for the post office. They are for YOU to specify passport type and endorsement.

Passport type codes (22 CFR 51.3):
D = Diplomatic  |  O = Official  |  S = Service  |  NFR = No Fee Regular  |  R = Regular (default)

Endorsement codes (Foreign Affairs Manual 5052):
09 – Bearer is a United States national and NOT a United States citizen (applies to all passport types)
11 – Bearer is an Ambassador at Large for [your cause/nation] (diplomatic only, but you can request it)
00 – No special endorsement
