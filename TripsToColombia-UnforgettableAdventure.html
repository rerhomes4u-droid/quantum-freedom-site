A Quarter-Century of Conscious Travel

Since 2000, our community has rewritten the story of tourism in Colombia.

2000 - The Seed

A simple idea: What if travelers could directly fund a child's school supplies just by mentioning a website? The first 10% donation from a Salento coffee tour was made.

2008 - The Network

100+ providers across Quindío, Risaralda, and Caldas join. The model proves that shared prosperity creates the most loyal partnerships.

2015 - The Turning Point

Medical tourism is integrated. A portion of every surgery and wellness treatment now also funds local health clinics. The ecosystem expands beyond "tourism."

2020 - The Digital Covenant

The launch of the transparent, trackable 10% model. Every transaction's impact is recorded. The person who refers the provider shares in the 10%, creating viral, organic growth.

Today - The Ecosystem

Thousands of providers, from Amazon guides to Cartagena chefs and Medellín surgeons, are part of the covenant. You can now track the school, family, or cause your adventure supports.
