📜 Citizenship Law
State Citizen
  • Article I, §10: “No State shall make any Thing but gold and silver Coin a Tender…”
  • Direct access to the Bill of Rights (9th & 10th Amendments).
  • Cannot be prosecuted by information (Hurtado v. California).
US / 14th Amendment Citizen
  • 31 U.S.C. §5103: Legal tender (Federal Reserve Notes).
  • Bill of Rights only through Incorporation Doctrine.
  • Tied to the District of Columbia, not to a State.

Key Cases: Slaughter-House, Bradwell, Paul v. Virginia, Twining v. New Jersey.

⏳ The 30‑Day Rule

Under CCP §2033.280, failure to respond to Requests for Admissions within 30 days results in automatic admission of each matter. This is a powerful weapon.

If they serve objections only (copy‑paste), those objections are waived. You can move to compel and the Court will deem the matters admitted.

📌 Key Rules
  • Ind. Trial Rule 53.1(F): If a judge fails to rule within 30 days, file a praecipe to send the matter to the Supreme Court.
  • Judicial Canons: A judge must be impartial, timely, and competent. If they violate the Canons, file a grievance.
  • Rule 23 (Professional Conduct): Attorneys who violate the rules can be reported to the State Bar.
