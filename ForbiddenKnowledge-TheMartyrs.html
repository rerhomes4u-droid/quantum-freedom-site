03Methodology

This catalogue was assembled from primary court records, correspondence, contemporary chronicles, and institutional archives. Each case is cross-referenced against at least two independent sources where available.

⚠ Epistemic Gap

For 5 cases, the suppression was so complete that no primary record survives. These are reconstructed from marginalia, later references, and institutional silence itself — the absence of expected documents is treated as evidence.

Verdicts are classified as Verified (strong documentary evidence), Unverified (single-source or fragmentary), Disputed (competing interpretations), or Suppressed (evidence deliberately destroyed or classified).
