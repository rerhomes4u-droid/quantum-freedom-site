About Scutum Fidei

Scutum Fidei is Latin for "Shield of Faith." It is a spiritual tool for the laity — a collection of Catholic prayers, a tactical binding module, and a community prayer board.

The Daily Armory contains the foundational prayers of the faith: the Sign of the Cross, the Our Father, the Hail Mary, the Glory Be, the Apostles' Creed, the Act of Contrition, the Anima Christi, the Prayer to St. Michael, the Sub Tuum Praesidium, and the Sealing Prayer.

The Tactical Module generates a binding prayer for a specific spirit and a specific person. It is based on the Catholic deliverance tradition. It is not a replacement for the sacraments or for professional deliverance ministry. It is a tool for the laity.

The Community Prayers board saves your prayer requests in your browser. They are private to this device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can delete any prayer at any time.
