THE FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE

The Constitution establishes a fundamental separation: The State cannot be both the accuser and the judge.

"It is a violation of due process for the same sovereign to both prosecute and adjudicate a case against an individual."
- Constitutional Principle

When you are accused of a crime by the State, that same State cannot then sit in judgment of its own accusation.

This is not a technicality. This is the bedrock of American liberty - a protection against tyranny encoded in our founding document.

THE CONSTITUTIONAL VIOLATION

When a State brings charges against you, that State becomes a party to the case. The Constitution then requires that a neutral, separate sovereign (the federal judiciary) adjudicate the matter.

State courts lack jurisdiction over cases where the State itself is a party, because:

  • The State cannot be an impartial arbiter of its own case
  • This violates due process under the 5th and 14th Amendments
  • It constitutes a breach of the separation of powers
JUDICIAL TREASON

When a state judge asserts jurisdiction over a case where the State is a party, they are acting:

"WITHOUT JURISDICTION"

Under constitutional law, this constitutes an act of treason against the Constitution they swore to uphold.

CASE LAW AUTHORITY
U.S. v. Will, 449 U.S. 200, 216 (1980)

"Whenever a judge acts where he/she does not have jurisdiction to act, the judge is engaged in an act of treason."

Cohens v. Virginia, 19 U.S. (6 Wheat) 264, 404 (1821)

"The judicial power of the United States extends to all cases arising under the Constitution... to controversies to which the United States shall be a party."

18 U.S.C. Section 2382 - Misprision of Treason

"Whoever, owing allegiance to the United States and having knowledge of the commission of any treason against them, conceals and does not... disclose and make known the same to the President or to a judge of the United States... is guilty of misprision of treason."

This means attorneys and other judges who witness this constitutional violation but say nothing are themselves guilty of misprision of treason.
