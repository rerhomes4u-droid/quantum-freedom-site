Microwave Syndrome (Electromagnetic Hypersensitivity)

A 2024 paper in Reviews on Environmental Health by Lennart Hardell and Mona Nilsson documented 16 people, aged 4 to 83, who developed microwave syndrome within a short time after being exposed to 5G base stations close to their dwellings.

The symptoms: sleeping difficulty, headache, fatigue, irritability, concentration problems, loss of immediate memory, emotional distress, depression tendency, anxiety, panic, dysesthesia (unusual touch-based sensations), burning and lancinating skin, cardiovascular symptoms (transitory high or irregular pulse), dyspnea, and pain in muscles and joints.

In most cases, the symptoms declined and disappeared within a short time period after the studied persons moved to a place with no 5G.

Source: Hardell, L., & Nilsson, M. (2024). "Case Report: The Microwave Syndrome after 5G Deployment." Reviews on Environmental Health.
