💵 Money ≠ Federal Reserve Notes

📖 U.S. Const. Art. I §10: “No State shall make anything but gold and silver coin a tender in payment of debts.”
📖 Black’s Law Dictionary – Money: “Gold, silver, or paper money used as circulating medium of exchange. Does NOT embrace notes, bonds, evidences of debt.”
🔥 Federal Reserve Note: A note. An evidence of debt. Legally NOT money.
📜 UCC §3-104 – Negotiable Instrument defined: “An unconditional promise to pay or unconditional order to pay a fixed amount of money, if it is payable to bearer or to order at the time it is issued.”
✅ Two types: Promissory note (promise to pay) and Bill of exchange (order to pay).
✅ Must be: (1) unconditional promise/order; (2) fixed amount; (3) payable to bearer/order; (4) signed.

“A utility bill is a bill of exchange (negotiable instrument). A mortgage is a promissory note (negotiable instrument). Your signature is the magic that creates value.”

✍️ Blank Endorsement – Why You Fund Your Own Loans

🔓 Blank endorsement: When you sign a negotiable instrument without specifying a payee. The instrument becomes payable to BEARER (anyone who holds it).
💣 “When they slide the mortgage documents across the table and you sign without ‘pay to the order of…’ you are abandoning that security. You are donating it to whoever picks it up.”

What the bank does: They take your blank‑endorsed note, stamp a special endorsement (“Pay to the order of [Bank Name], without recourse”), then take it to the Federal Reserve discount window. They swap your note for Federal Reserve notes. They then lend you back your own money at interest.

✍️ Blank endorsement (what most people do):
[Signature] — no payee — “payable to bearer” → you abandoned it.
✍️ Special endorsement (what you should do):
“Without recourse / Pay to the order of [Your Trust or ALL CAPS name] / [Signature]” → you claim the proceeds.

🏦 The Federal Reserve Discount Window – 12 USC §412

📜 12 USC §412 (Note issues): “Upon application accompanied with a tender of collateral security… the Federal Reserve agent shall deliver to the bank… Federal Reserve notes equal to the sum of the collateral.”

The swap explained: Your signed promissory note (collateral) is exchanged for Federal Reserve notes. The bank takes your note to the window, gets FRNs, then gives you a portion (the “loan”) and pockets the difference (the “interest”).

🔑 “Without recourse” meaning (UCC §3-415): “Without recourse” disclaims any liability to subsequent holders. If the instrument says “without recourse,” the endorser is NOT liable if the instrument is dishonored. Therefore, when you endorse “without recourse,” you never have to pay the Federal Reserve back.
