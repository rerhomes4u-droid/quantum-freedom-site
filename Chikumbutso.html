01The Claim

What Is Being Said

Maxwell Chikumbutso, a Zimbabwean inventor, has created a "Microsonic Energy Device" that captures ambient radio frequency waves and converts them into usable electricity. The device powers vehicles and generators without fuel, without charging, and without any external input. He demonstrated it to President Emmerson Mnangagwa at State House in January 2025. The government praised him. Then the government deleted the posts. The alternative channels say he is being suppressed by the energy cartels.

The Record

The demonstrations happened. Chikumbutso appeared at State House on January 28, 2025, with a vehicle and a motorcycle he claimed were powered by radio waves. President Mnangagwa called him "so gifted that you will not believe it" [citation:12]. The presidential communications account posted a video and a statement praising the "groundbreaking" invention [citation:6].

The posts were deleted. Within days, the accounts belonging to President Mnangagwa, the Ministry of Information, and the Permanent Secretary deleted all posts regarding Chikumbutso and his invention [citation:6]. The government backtracked. The praise became silence.

The physics does not work. Radio frequency waves are measured in nanovolts. They do not carry enough usable energy to power a vehicle. The laws of thermodynamics apply. The energy captured would be insufficient to overcome the energy required for the conversion process itself, let alone propel a car [citation:13].

The device was a solar station. Journalist Hopewell Chin'ono performed a reverse image search on the device Chikumbutso demonstrated to the President. It led to an Amazon listing for a portable solar power station sold by Browey for $109.99 [citation:6].

The car was a Chinese SUV. Engineer Hatach Mujumi found that the vehicle Chikumbutso claimed to have invented was a Kaiyi X3 Pro, an electric SUV sold for $18,500 on Made-in-China.com [citation:6].

The suppressed claim is not about the device. It is about the hope. The hope is that Africa can innovate. The hope is that free energy exists. The hope is that the cartels are afraid. The hope is that the story is true.

02The Criminal History

What the Record Shows

Maxwell Chikumbutso was arrested in 2018 on six counts of armed robbery. He was denied bail. The ZimEye account claims he is a "convicted fraudster." The record contains the arrest. It contains the bail denial. It contains the allegations. The reader decides what it means.

The Record

The arrest is documented. Chikumbutso was arrested in 2018 on six counts of armed robbery, along with five accomplices [citation:3]. During his bail hearing, he claimed to own substantial assets, including immovable property. Justice Roger Manyangadze Foroma noted that "not a single deed of transfer was produced or referred to as proof" [citation:3].

The bail was denied. The State opposed bail, arguing that a firearm recovered by police belonged to Chikumbutso. The firearm was found in the vehicle of a co-accused, who claimed he had given Chikumbutso a lift [citation:3]. The judge was not satisfied that the possession of the firearm was innocent [citation:3].

The trial resumed in 2021. Some of the accomplices had passed away. The group was accused of robbing Murefu Service Station in Waterfalls, Harare [citation:3].

The ZimEye claims are serious. The account claims Chikumbutso is a "convicted fraudster" and that court records have disappeared from the archive [citation:8][citation:15]. It lists specific cases: HC 1234/20 (fraud conviction upheld) and CRB 4321/18 (theft charge, found not guilty) [citation:8]. These claims are contested. They are in the record. The reader decides.

03The Physics

What the Record Shows

Radio frequency waves do not carry enough energy to power a vehicle. This is not a conspiracy. It is physics.

The Record

Ambient RF is real. Radio frequency energy exists in the environment. It can power small sensors. It can be harvested at very small scales [citation:2].

The scale is the problem. A moving vehicle requires kilowatts of power. Ambient RF provides microwatts. The difference is not a matter of engineering. It is a matter of orders of magnitude [citation:13].

The thermodynamics. Converting RF to electricity involves losses. The energy captured would be insufficient to overcome the energy required for the conversion process itself, let alone propel a car. The claim that the device "violates the laws of physics" is not a mark of genius. It is a mark of impossibility [citation:13].
