The Dispute Resolution Protocol

The PMA does not go to court. The PMA resolves disputes privately. The parties agree to binding arbitration. The arbitrator is chosen by the parties. The arbitration is binding. The protocol is the agreement.

  • 1

    Notice of Dispute

    The party with the grievance sends a written notice to the other party. The notice states the specific claim, the specific harm, and the specific remedy requested.

  • 2

    Selection of Arbitrator

    The parties select an arbitrator. The arbitrator is a member of the PMA in good standing. If the parties cannot agree, the trustee selects the arbitrator.

  • 3

    Arbitration Hearing

    The hearing is private. The parties present their evidence. The arbitrator hears both sides. The arbitrator issues a written decision.

  • 4

    Binding Decision

    The decision is binding. The parties agree to abide by it. The decision is recorded in the PMA's minutes.

The Treasury

The PMA's treasury is the trust account. It is separate from the members' personal accounts. It is managed by the treasurer. It is audited by the trustees. It is reported to the members.

The Treasury Structure

1. The Trust Account. The PMA holds its funds in a trust account. The account is in the name of the trust. The trustee is the signatory.

2. The Public Ledger. Every donation is recorded. Every expenditure is recorded. The ledger is public to the members.

3. The Mutual Aid Fund. A percentage of every donation goes to the mutual aid fund. The fund is for members in crisis.

4. The Audit. The trustees audit the treasury annually. The audit is reported to the members.

The Assembly Rules

The assembly is the governing body of the PMA. It meets regularly. It votes on resolutions. It elects the trustees. It amends the bylaws. The rules are the procedure.

  • 1

    Quorum

    A quorum is required for any binding vote. The quorum is defined in the bylaws. If the quorum is not met, the meeting is adjourned.

  • 2

    Voting

    Every member in good standing has one vote. The vote is recorded in the minutes. The majority decides, unless the bylaws require a supermajority.

  • 3

    Minutes

    The secretary records the minutes. The minutes include the date, the attendees, the resolutions, the votes, and the decisions. The minutes are kept in the PMA's archive.

  • 4

    Amendments

    The bylaws can be amended by a supermajority vote. The amendment is proposed in writing. The amendment is voted on at the next meeting.

The Succession Plan

The PMA is not a person. It is a covenant. The covenant survives the founder. The succession plan is the proof that the nation is bigger than any one member.

  • 1

    The Trustees

    The PMA has three trustees. Two must agree on any major decision. If one is incapacitated, the other two continue.

  • 2

    The Designated Successor

    The founder designates a successor in writing. The successor is a member in good standing. The successor is approved by the trustees.

  • 3

    The Keys

    The founder's digital keys, passwords, and access are held by the designated successor and one trustee. The deadman switch releases the archive if the founder does not check in.

  • 4

    The Covenant

    Every member signs the covenant. The covenant is the constitution. It does not change with the founder.
