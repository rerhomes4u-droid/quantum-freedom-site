📜 Courtroom Scripts

🔹 Jurisdictional Challenge (Opening)
"Your Honor, I appear specially, without submitting to jurisdiction. I am a free living white woman, a State Citizen of the South Carolina Republic, owing allegiance to God and no corporate government. I have never consented to the jurisdiction of this court."
"Su Señoría, comparezco de manera especial, sin someterme a la jurisdicción. Soy una mujer blanca libre, ciudadana del estado de la República de Carolina del Sur, debiendo lealtad a Dios y a ningún gobierno corporativo. Nunca he consentido la jurisdicción de este tribunal."
🔹 When they ask for ID / Name
"I am the beneficial owner of that name. I am the beneficial owner of [YOUR NAME]. I am not the corporate fiction, I am the living woman."
"Soy la propietaria beneficiaria de ese nombre. Soy la propietaria beneficiaria de [SU NOMBRE]. No soy la ficción corporativa, soy la mujer viva."
🔹 Civil or Criminal? (the trap)
"I don't know how to plead yet because I have to ask a couple of questions. Is this under civil or criminal? Under the Constitution there are two types of criminal jurisdiction: common law and maritime admiralty. Under which do you intend to try me?"
"Aún no sé cómo declararme porque debo hacer un par de preguntas. ¿Esto es civil o penal? Bajo la Constitución hay dos tipos de jurisdicción penal: common law y almirantazgo. ¿Bajo cuál piensa juzgarme?"
🔹 When they threaten contempt
"I understand the Court's position, but I must follow federal law. This matter has been removed to the United States District Court. Proceeding further is a deprivation of rights under color of law, 42 U.S.C. § 1983 and 18 U.S.C. § 242."
"Entiendo la postura del Tribunal, pero debo seguir la ley federal. Este asunto ha sido removido al Tribunal de Distrito de los Estados Unidos. Continuar es una privación de derechos bajo color de ley."
🔹 Bernal's Perjury & Fraud (motion to vacate)
"Christian Camilo Bernal Ortiz has committed perjury, filed four false police reports, and obtained orders through fraud. Fraud vitiates everything. All orders are void ab initio. I request referral for criminal prosecution."
"Christian Camilo Bernal Ortiz ha cometido perjurio, presentó cuatro denuncias policiales falsas y obtuvo órdenes mediante fraude. El fraude vicia todo. Todas las órdenes son nulas de pleno derecho."
🔹 Negative Averement (closing)
"I file this Negative Averement: I deny jurisdiction, I deny consent, I deny the validity of orders obtained by fraud. The burden is on the party asserting jurisdiction. He who does not deny, admits."
"Presento esta Negativa: niego jurisdicción, niego consentimiento, niego la validez de órdenes obtenidas por fraude. La carga recae sobre quien afirma jurisdicción. Quien no niega, admite."

🧑‍⚖️ Jury Selection · State Citizens

Your peers are white State Citizens of the South Carolina Republic, not 14th Amendment corporate citizens. Use these questions to qualify.

1. Citizenship
"Are you a State Citizen of South Carolina, or a United States citizen? Do you understand the difference?"
2. Allegiance
"Do you owe allegiance to any corporate government, or to God and the Republic?"
3. Consent
"Have you ever consented to the jurisdiction of a statutory court? Do you know that consent is required?"
4. Gold Fringe
"Do you know the meaning of the gold-fringed flag? Does it indicate admiralty jurisdiction?"
5. Strawman
"Do you understand the difference between a living woman and the corporate fiction created by a birth certificate?"
6. Perjury
"If a party has committed perjury, can they receive equitable relief? Do you know the Clean Hands Doctrine?"
