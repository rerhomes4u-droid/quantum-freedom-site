📡 FOIA REQUEST — How to Collect Evidence Without Begging

Freedom of Information Act (5 USC §552): Agencies MUST respond within 20 days (often less). Use FOIA to verify if your Revocation of Election, Novation, or W-8BEN was processed. Don’t call and beg — send a FOIA request. Keep paper trail for litigation.

📄 SAMPLE FOIA REQUEST (short, unemotional, direct):
“To: [Agency FOIA Office]. Pursuant to 5 USC §552, I request all records, correspondence, and documents related to [your name / your Revocation of Election certified mail #]. Please provide copies within statutory timeframe. Thank you.”

Tips: Fax or certified mail (paper trail). If they say “no records,” rephrase wording slightly and resubmit. Costs under $50 are often waived. Use FOIA to build your evidence binder before litigation.
