The Removal Process

1
Go to State Court

Visit the state court where your case is currently pending.

  • Find the clerk's office
  • Locate your case file
  • Request the criminal complaint
2
Get Certified Copy

Ask the clerk for a certified copy of the criminal complaint.

  • It's a piece of paper with your name and the state's name
  • The clerk will stamp/seal it to certify it
  • Cost: Usually around $5 (varies by jurisdiction)
3
Go to Federal Court

Take your certified copy to the nearest Federal District Court.

  • Find the Clerk's Office
  • Bring ALL paperwork from state court
  • Get a Notice of Removal form
4
File Notice of Removal

Complete and file the Notice of Removal citing:

28 U.S.C. § 1441 - Removal of civil actions
28 U.S.C. § 1446(e) - Once removed, state court loses jurisdiction until remanded

This is NOT a motion - it's AUTOMATIC.

5
Submit Complete Packet

Hand in everything as one complete packet:

  • Notice of Removal
  • Certified copy of state complaint
  • Civil Cover Sheet
  • All other state court paperwork

The federal clerk will assign a case number.

6
Notify State Court

You MUST formally notify the state court that the case has been removed:

  • Serve the STATE COURT CLERK
  • Serve the STATE COURT JUDGE
  • Serve the PROSECUTOR

Use certified mail with return receipt for proof of service.

⚠️ CRITICAL INFORMATION

Once removed to federal court, the state court has NO JURISDICTION until the case is remanded back.
