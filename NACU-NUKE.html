📜 The Law of Citizenship
State Citizen
  • Article I, §10: “No State shall make any Thing but gold and silver Coin a Tender in Payment of Debts”
  • Direct access to the Bill of Rights (9th & 10th Amendments).
  • Governed by the 1849 Constitution (for California).
  • Cannot be prosecuted by information (Hurtado v. California).
US / 14th Amendment Citizen
  • 31 U.S.C. §5103: Legal tender (Federal Reserve Notes).
  • Access to Bill of Rights only through Incorporation Doctrine.
  • Governed by the 1879 Constitution (for California).
  • Tied to the District of Columbia, not to a State.

Key Cases: Slaughter-House Cases (83 U.S. 36), Bradwell v. Illinois (83 U.S. 130), Paul v. Virginia (75 U.S. 168), Twining v. New Jersey (211 U.S. 78).

⏳ The 30‑Day Rule

Under CCP §2033.280, failure to respond to Requests for Admissions within 30 days results in automatic admission of each matter. This is a powerful weapon.

If they serve objections only (copy‑paste), those objections are waived. You can move to compel and the Court will deem the matters admitted.

📌 Key Rules
  • Indiana Trial Rule 53.1(F): If a judge fails to rule within 30 days, you may file a praecipe to send the matter to the Supreme Court.
  • Judicial Canons: A judge must be impartial, timely, and competent. If they violate the Canons, file a grievance with the Judicial Qualifications Commission.
  • Rule 23 (Professional Conduct): Attorneys who violate the rules (e.g., failing to file an appearance) can be reported to the State Bar.
