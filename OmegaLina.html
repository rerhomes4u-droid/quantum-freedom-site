2

The Bible Jesus Actually Used

Let's look at what Scripture Jesus and His disciples actually read and quoted:

📖 Historical Fact:

In the 1st century, most Jews (including Jesus and His disciples) spoke Greek as their common language. The Septuagint (LXX) was the Greek translation of Hebrew Scriptures that was widely used.

🔍 Scriptural Evidence:

When the New Testament quotes the Old Testament, over 90% of the quotes come from the Septuagint, not from Hebrew manuscripts. Jesus Himself quoted from the Greek Septuagint.

Example: In Matthew 1:23, the quote from Isaiah 7:14 uses the Septuagint's Greek word "παρθένος" (parthenos) meaning "virgin," which aligns perfectly with the virgin birth prophecy.

3

The Name That Saved Millions

Let's gently examine the name issue with historical care:

📜 Linguistic Fact:

The "J" argument is misleading. In ancient languages, sounds matter more than letters. The Greek "Ιησούς" (Iēsous) was pronounced "Yay-soos," which naturally became "Jesus" in English as language evolved.

🎯 Historical Consistency:

For 2,000 years, believers worldwide have been saved calling on the name Jesus in their own languages. The Holy Spirit honors the heart, not just specific sounds.

Biblical Truth: "Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved." (Acts 4:12)

That name, in the original Greek text, is Ιησούς (Iēsous/Jesus).

Notice: If the "J" argument were valid, then names like Jeremiah, Joshua, Joseph, Jacob and Judah would also be "wrong." Yet Hebrew Roots advocates only target the name Jesus.

4

What Historical Sources Reveal

📚 Jewish Historian Evidence:

Flavius Josephus (37-100 AD), a Jewish priest and historian who lived during Jesus' time, wrote about Him in his historical works. Josephus calls Him "Jesus" (in Greek: Ιησούς), not "Yeshua."

✍️ Manuscript Evidence:

Every single New Testament manuscript (over 5,800 Greek manuscripts) uses Ιησούς (Iēsous). None use "Yeshua." The earliest fragments (from the 2nd century) all say Ιησούς.

🎭 The "Yeshua" Origin:

The specific spelling "Yeshua" being promoted today comes from medieval Jewish mystical texts (Kabbalah/Zohar), not from 1st century Christianity.

6

The Real Historical Hebrew

🗣️ Language Reality:

By Jesus' time, Hebrew was essentially a dead language for common use. Most Jews spoke Aramaic or Greek. The "Hebrew" promoted today was reconstructed in the Middle Ages.

📖 The Apostles' Approach:

The Holy Spirit came at Pentecost and people heard the gospel in their own languages (Acts 2:6). God always meets people where they are linguistically.

The early Church never required people to learn Hebrew or use Hebrew names. They preached Jesus in Greek (the common language) and translated Scripture into local languages.
