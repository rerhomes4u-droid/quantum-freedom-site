🏆 HIS ACHIEVEMENTS

Gustavo Petro's government delivered concrete results for the Colombian people. These are not promises. These are facts.

3.8M

Colombians Lifted from Poverty

Monetary poverty fell from 36.6% in 2022 to 28% in 2025 — the lowest level in Colombia's history. Nearly 4 million people escaped poverty. Extreme poverty fell from 13.8% to 9.6% [citation:33].

381,991

Hectares of Land Returned to Campesinos

Through the Agrarian Reform, over 381,000 hectares were delivered to campesino and ethnic communities — benefiting over 100,000 families. This was a historic debt finally paid [citation:38][citation:24].

23%

Minimum Wage Increase

Petro raised the minimum wage by 23% for his final year — far above the 5.1% inflation — directly benefiting 2.5 million workers and their families [citation:33].

23%

Salary Increase for Soldiers and Police

Soldiers, police, and the lowest ranks received increases of up to 23%, while high-ranking officers received 7%. The wage gap was reduced, and the dignity of the troops was restored [citation:2][citation:11].

70%

Bonuses for Young Soldiers

Bonuses for soldiers doing mandatory service rose from 30% to 70% of minimum wage, and were set to reach 100% in 2026 — lifting families out of extreme poverty [citation:15].

27%

Police Budget Increase

The police budget was increased by 27%, with a 10% average annual salary increase and a 100% bonus increase for auxiliaries [citation:3].

🚢 THE HOSPITAL SHIPS

When the people of the Pacific Coast were forgotten, Petro sent hospitals. On water.

Benkos Biohó — The First Hospital Ship in Colombian History

Built by Colombian hands with an investment of over 87 billion pesos. The ship brings medium and high-complexity medical services to 22 coastal municipalities in Chocó, Valle del Cauca, Cauca, and Nariño. It serves over 150,000 people who previously had no access to dignified healthcare [citation:1][citation:6][citation:14].

On board: surgery room, radiology, ultrasound, mammography, four recovery rooms with stretchers, an incubator, a pharmacy, and a helipad. The crew includes 35 professionals — doctors, specialists, and maritime staff [citation:37].

Marisol's Story

Marisol de la Cruz, a survivor of forced recruitment and sexual violence, lives in a stilt house in Buenaventura. Before the ship, she had to cancel gynecology appointments because traveling to Cali cost 500,000 pesos — money she didn't have. Now, the doctor comes to her. "This ship brings blessings," she says. "It brings life" [citation:10].

Petro called it "the greatest effort" of his government toward the Pacific. And he was right.

🇵🇸 HIS STAND FOR PALESTINE

This is what they cannot forgive. This is what cost him so dearly. He refused to be silent about genocide.

— Gustavo Petro [citation:28]

"I am ashamed of a Government and a Congress that accepts a diplomatic relationship with the one who has killed 22,000 babies of humanity with missiles. The one who laughs at a genocide is a fascist."

"No honorable man or woman in Colombia can accept applauding genocide."

— Gustavo Petro [citation:20]

Petro didn't just speak. He acted:

  • Broke diplomatic relations with Israel on May 2, 2024 [citation:21]
  • Intervened in the ICJ case brought by South Africa against Israel for genocide [citation:21]
  • Opened a Colombian embassy in Ramallah — the first in history [citation:27]

Petro stood for Palestine when it cost Colombia dearly. He knew it would. He did it anyway.

🚨 THE THREATS AGAINST HIS LIFE

🚁 The Helicopter Attack

In February 2026, Petro's helicopter was forced to divert over open sea for four hours after intelligence indicated it would be shot at. His children were on board. "I didn't land where I was supposed to because I had information that they were going to shoot the helicopter, with my children also on board," he said [citation:22][citation:29].

💉 The Poison Plot

Petro revealed that a retired police general had received orders to introduce psychoactive substances into his vehicle to link him to drug trafficking and sabotage a high-level meeting [citation:22][citation:36].

🔫 The Drug Traffickers' Hit

Petro has stated that an international "junta of drug traffickers" has tried to assassinate him multiple times since 2022 [citation:36].
