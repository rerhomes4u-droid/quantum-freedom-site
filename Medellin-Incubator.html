The HELOC method — from his own e-course, Chapter 8

Get a Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC). Use it to pay off all your outstanding credit card balances. Apply what's left to your mortgage principal. Then deposit your entire paycheck into the HELOC each month. Pay for everything with a zero-balance credit card. At the end of the month, write a check from the HELOC to your credit card.

Because the HELOC uses daily amortization, every day your money is parked there, it replenishes itself. When the HELOC balance reaches its limit again, write another check directly to your mortgage principal. Repeat the process.

"Repeat the process and you'll soon see that your mortgage is nothing but a distant memory."
