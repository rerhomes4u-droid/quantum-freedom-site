Case 1: New York Hush Money

Donald Trump was convicted on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records related to a 2016 payment to Stormy Daniels. He received an unconditional discharge on January 10, 2025, but the conviction remains on his record and is subject to appeal.

Details: The trial began on April 15, 2024, and resulted in a guilty verdict on May 30, 2024. Sentencing was delayed and eventually set for January 10, 2025, where Trump received an unconditional discharge, meaning no prison time, fines, or probation. He is appealing the conviction.

Case 2: Georgia Election Interference

Trump was indicted in August 2023 on multiple counts related to efforts to overturn Georgia’s 2020 election results. The case was dismissed on November 26, 2025, after several legal twists and procedural moves.

Details: The case involved charges of criminal acts to overturn the election, including soliciting false electors. After several legal challenges, including the disqualification of the prosecutor, the case was dismissed by a judge in November 2025.
