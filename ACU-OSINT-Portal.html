AMBASSADOR MISSION BRIEFING

Our Purpose

The ACU International OSINT Agency provides legal, ethical decision support to ambassadors and diplomatic personnel worldwide. We transform publicly available information into actionable intelligence for humanitarian missions, conflict prevention, and global unity initiatives.

Strategic Position

We operate in the 1-4% information gap - the vast majority of publicly available information that traditional agencies overlook. Our network of Citizen Intelligence Minutemen provides ground truth that satellite imagery and signals intelligence cannot capture.

Value Proposition

While traditional intelligence agencies focus on state secrets, we focus on public knowledge for public good. Our ambassadors receive verified, contextualized information about local conditions, cultural nuances, and emerging opportunities for dialogue.

LEGAL & ETHICAL FOUNDATIONS

All ACU OSINT operations are conducted within strict legal boundaries, respecting international law, privacy regulations, and diplomatic protocols.

INTERNATIONAL LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Understanding the legal boundaries and authorities for OSINT operations

Operational Mandate

The ACU International OSINT Agency operates under:

  • UN Principles on Open Source Information
  • International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation
  • Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations
  • Customary International Law on Information Gathering

Prohibited Activities

What we NEVER do:

  • Access non-public information
  • Impersonate or use false pretenses
  • Bypass authentication systems
  • Conduct surveillance on individuals
  • Violate terms of service agreements
  • Engage in any form of hacking

Approved Methodologies

What we ALWAYS do:

  • Monitor publicly available media
  • Analyze open government data
  • Review academic publications
  • Examine commercial registries
  • Study satellite imagery archives
  • Analyze social media (public posts only)

Diplomatic Immunity & OSINT

Ambassadors using ACU OSINT tools remain protected by diplomatic immunity while conducting information gathering activities, provided they:

  1. Only access publicly available information
  2. Respect local laws and customs
  3. Do not engage in commercial espionage
  4. Maintain proper documentation of sources
  5. Report findings through official diplomatic channels
