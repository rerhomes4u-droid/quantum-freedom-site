WHAT IF YOU ARE
ALREADY DEAD?

Not metaphorically. Not spiritually. What if the "you" reading this is just a ghost? A collection of electrical patterns that have already dissolved and reformed a million times?

"If you are already dead, what is there to lose?"
The fear of death is the leash.
Every fear you have is a child of it.
BREATHE
Do not sleep. Just notice.
Notice that you are not controlling the breath. It is happening to you.
When you were five years old, you had a name, a face, a set of fears.

Where is that five-year-old now?
That person is dead. Dissolved into the past.
The cells replaced. The memories rewritten.
You died a thousand times. And yet—here you are.

You are not a being having an experience.
You are experience being momentarily mistaken for a being.

The system cannot threaten a ghost.
The system cannot imprison a memory.
The system cannot tax a pattern of electricity.
