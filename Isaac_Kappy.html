A Legend for the Children

Who Isaac Kappy was

Isaac Kappy was an American actor best known for his role as the pet store owner in Thor (2011) and appearances in Terminator Salvation and Breaking Bad. But his true legacy was not in Hollywood — it was in his final years, when he became one of the first and most vocal whistleblowers about systemic child abuse and pedophilia in Hollywood and the U.S. government.

Born Isaac Benjamin Kappy on February 17, 1977, in Albuquerque, New Mexico, he was a natural performer who studied at the Oxford School of Drama in England. But when he learned about the abuse happening to children in the entertainment industry, he could no longer stay silent. In 2018 and 2019, he released a series of videos exposing what he called a "Satanic cult" operating at the highest levels of power.

⚠️ The Circumstances

On May 13, 2019, Isaac Kappy died after allegedly jumping from the Transwestern Road bridge in Albuquerque, New Mexico. He was 42 years old. According to the official narrative, he "fell or jumped" and was subsequently struck by a vehicle on Interstate 40. His death was ruled a suicide. However, Isaac had repeatedly stated in his videos that he was being harassed, followed, and threatened — and that if he died, it would not be by his own hand.

His Most Important Work

The exposés that made him a target

🎬 Hollywood Pedophilia Network

Isaac publicly accused multiple celebrities of child abuse, including Tom Hanks, Steven Spielberg, Seth Green, Dan Schneider, and Stephen Colbert. He claimed he had witnessed or had direct knowledge of abuse at the highest levels of the entertainment industry.

Names Named

🎭 Seth Green & the "Private Club"

In one of his most specific allegations, Isaac claimed that Seth Green had invited him to a "private club" where he was asked to do things he refused to do. He also stated that Seth Green was involved in the abuse of children. This claim was one of the most widely discussed and disputed of his disclosures.

Detailed Allegations

🕍 The Satanic Cult at the Top

Isaac repeatedly stated that the abuse was not random — it was organized, ritualistic, and Satanic. He claimed that a "Satanic cult" operated at the highest levels of power, and that children were used in rituals to gain power and control over participants through blackmail.

Ritual Abuse

👑 The Rothschilds & Cannibalism

Isaac claimed the Rothschilds were involved in cannibalism — a claim that was met with widespread ridicule but which he maintained until his death. He said this was part of the same system of ritual abuse used to control the elite.

Controversial Claim

🏛️ Government Involvement

Isaac stated that child trafficking and abuse were not just a Hollywood problem — they were a government problem. He claimed that intelligence agencies were involved in trafficking children and using them for blackmail operations.

Government Complicity

🏰 The Royal Family Cover-Up

Isaac exposed the cover-up of Jimmy Savile's crimes by Mark Thompson (then Director-General of the BBC and later CEO of The New York Times) and the Royal Family. He pointed out that Savile's crimes were known for decades but were suppressed by those in power.

UK Connections

🎥 The 2018 Video Series

In 2018, Isaac released a series of videos in which he systematically laid out his claims. These videos were viewed millions of times and sparked a global conversation about pedophilia in Hollywood. They were also the reason he was targeted, he said.

Primary Source

⚠️ Death Threats & Harassment

Isaac repeatedly stated that he was being followed, threatened, and harassed. He said his phone was tapped, his movements were tracked, and that he was in constant fear for his life. He told his audience: "If I die, it wasn't suicide."

Pre-Death Warning

"I know too much. I've seen too much. And I'm not going to be silent. If they kill me, I want the world to know — it wasn't me. I would never take my own life. I love life too much. I love my daughter too much."

— Isaac Kappy, in one of his final videos
