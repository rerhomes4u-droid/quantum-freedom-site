📞 Equity Call

Simplified
⚖️
Without prejudice · UCC 1-308
All communications preserve your rights.
Done already? Tell them: “I have performed”
1

Start with kindness

“Well, thank you so much for calling. Who am I speaking with?”
“Do you have an employee ID number? What’s the company you work for? What’s your mailing address? May I have your supervisor’s name?”
2

Let them know you performed

“I have already performed the equity on this matter. Please send me the written presentment and I will honor it immediately.”
“Does your record reflect that I have accepted and returned all presentments for settlement and closure?”
✅ This stops repeated calls — you already performed equity.
3

Request written presentment

“Thank you. If you could put that in writing and mail it to me, I will honor it immediately once I receive it. Could you do that for me?”
4

End gracefully

“Thank you so much, you have a wonderful day.”
⚖️ Equity · Without prejudice · UCC 1-308
This is a simplified tool. Speak calmly and kindly.
