📜 CONTRACT DEFINITION (Black's 4th, p.470)

“A promissory agreement between two or more persons that creates, modifies or destroys a legal relationship. An agreement upon sufficient consideration to do or not to do a particular thing.”

Obligation (p.1299): “An undertaking to perform. That which constitutes a legal or moral duty and renders a person liable to coercion for neglecting it.”
⚡ Payment (critical): “Fulfillment of a promise or performance of an agreement — NOT Federal Reserve notes.” Disconnect your mind from “green paper” — payment = performance of a duty or obligation.

🔥 CONSIDERATION (p.454): “The inducement to a contract. Something which is of value in the eye of the law moving from the plaintiff, either of benefit to the plaintiff or detriment to the defendant.”
⚠️ GROUND TRUTH: “Nothing is consideration that is not regarded as such by both parties.”

💡 If YOU do not regard a driver's license, SSN, “US citizen” status, or any license as providing benefit or utility → there is NO consideration → NO contract → VOID.

💰 VALUE (Black's p.1797) & ADEQUATE CONSIDERATION

Value: “Utility of an object in satisfying the needs or desires of human beings (value in use).”

Adequate consideration: “Equal or reasonably proportioned to the value; not so disproportionate as to shock morality and fair dealing.”

⚡ If a contract lacks adequate consideration → unconscionable / adhesion contract → unenforceable. You can sue the county for forcing a contract without consideration (involuntary servitude, 13th Amendment).

📉 UNILATERAL & ADHESION CONTRACTS

Unilateral contract: Only benefits one side (driver’s license, corporate “enfranchisement”). Not a true contract under common law; unenforceable.
Adhesion contract: Take-it-or-leave-it, unequal bargaining power (DMV forms, terms of service). Courts often refuse to enforce adhesion contracts when challenged with proper Novation.

🎯 Strategic play: Define what YOU consider consideration. If none → no meeting of the minds → contract evaporates.
