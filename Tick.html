PFIZER'S LYME DECEPTION — WHAT THE REPORTS SHOW

Lyme disease is the most common vector-borne illness in North America and Europe. The CDC estimates that approximately 476,000 people in the U.S. contract Lyme disease annually. This number has risen steadily over the past two decades.

⚠️ On-the-Ground Reports ⚠️

Wildlife observers across North America are documenting unprecedented tick infestations on deer, moose, birds, and even small mammals. These reports are widespread, though the cause remains under investigation.

🦟 THE TICK POPULATION EXPLOSION — WHAT OBSERVERS REPORT

Across North America and Europe, residents, hunters, and wildlife officials are reporting explosive increases in tick populations. In some areas, deer are found with hundreds of ticks. Moose are dying from tick infestations. Even birds and small mammals are overwhelmed.

📋 On-the-Ground Reports:

  • Moose in Maine, New Hampshire, and Minnesota — tick infestations causing severe anemia and death
  • Deer in the Northeast — visible tick burdens, with reports of "white-tailed deer with gray patches" (actually tick-covered)
  • Birds banded in tick-endemic areas — many found with heavy tick loads
  • Small mammals (mice, chipmunks) — reservoir hosts for Lyme bacteria, their populations also affected

Possible contributing factors (scientific consensus): climate change (warmer winters allow tick survival), reforestation (increased wildlife habitat), suburban development (human-wildlife interface), and wildlife population dynamics.

💉 PFIZER'S LYME VACCINE — THE CLINICAL TRIAL

Pfizer is developing a Lyme disease vaccine (VLA15) targeting the OspA protein. The mechanism: antibodies block the bacteria from leaving the tick to infect humans.

📋 Phase 3 VALOR Trial (Public Record):

  • 9,437 participants enrolled across US, Europe, Canada
  • Initial enrollment ~18,000; approximately half dropped due to "quality concerns with third-party clinical trial site operator"
  • Study missed statistical benchmark (15.8% vs required 20%)
  • Pfizer continues development despite missed benchmark

Pending litigation: Multiple shareholder lawsuits have been filed against Pfizer regarding disclosure of trial data. Oklahoma Firefighters Pension and Retirement System v. Pfizer, Inc. (2024) — alleges misrepresentation of clinical trial results and regulatory prospects for the Lyme vaccine.

🧬 THE OSPA MECHANISM — UNANSWERED QUESTIONS

The vaccine works by targeting OspA, a protein expressed by Borrelia burgdorferi while inside the tick. When a vaccinated human is bitten, antibodies enter the tick and block bacterial transmission.

⚠️ Scientific Gaps Noted in FDA Review:

  • Long-term ecological effects of vaccinated humans on tick populations unknown
  • Potential for selection pressure on Borrelia strains (escape mutants)
  • Autoimmune concerns (previous Lyme vaccine LYMErix was withdrawn due to arthritis claims)

The mechanism is elegant — but the long-term consequences of altering tick biology through human vaccination have not been adequately studied. Independent researchers have called for more ecological impact studies.

🛡️ WHAT YOU CAN DO — TICK PREVENTION PROTOCOLS

  • Permethrin-treated clothing (remains effective through multiple washes)
  • DEET or Picaridin repellents on exposed skin
  • Daily tick checks after outdoor activity
  • Shower within 2 hours of coming indoors
  • Landscape management — leaf litter removal, deer fencing, rodent control
  • Monitor tick populations in your area — report unusual findings to local extension services

If you find an attached tick: Remove carefully with fine-tipped tweezers. Clean the bite area. Monitor for rash or flu-like symptoms. Consult a physician if concerned.
