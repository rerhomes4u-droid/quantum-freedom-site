Key Distinction

You have TWO CITIZENSHIPS:

  1. United States Citizen - Natural born or naturalized person protected by the Constitution
  2. State Corporate Citizen - Maritime law "straw man" represented by your ALL CAPS name

The "corporate you" was created when you obtained a birth certificate, driver's license, or Social Security Number.

Actionable Declaration

When entering any courtroom or dealing with state authorities, you can file this declaration to preserve your constitutional rights:

UCC 1-207 Declaration

"I will not be compelled to perform under any contract either knowing or unknowing to me, in any way, for any reason. I will not do that. All rights reserved."

"I am a living breathing man/woman on the land and under the jurisdiction of the United States of America Republic. I do not recognize maritime jurisdiction over my natural person."

This declaration informs the maritime court that you will not be compelled to perform under contracts or codes.

Knowledge Base

Historical Timeline

1868
14th Amendment Ratified

Anyone born or naturalized in the United States is a citizen of the United States and also a citizen of the state wherein they reside.

1913
Federal Reserve Created

The U.S. monetary system shifted to debt-based currency. Citizens were placed under corporate codes through birth certificates and other documents.

1933
Emergency Banking Act

The United States declared bankruptcy and citizens became collateral. Birth certificates became securities traded on markets.

Present
Dual Citizenship System

Every U.S. citizen exists in two forms: the living person (common law) and the corporate entity (maritime/admiralty law).
