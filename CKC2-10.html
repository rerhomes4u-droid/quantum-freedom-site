🎯 Why a Private Motor Carrier DOT Number?

💡 The insight: The federal government (DOT) only has jurisdiction over COMMERCIAL activity. A private motor carrier hauls its own property, not for hire. If your vehicle is not used in commerce, you are NOT a “commercial motor vehicle” → NOT subject to state DMV registration schemes.

Two types of motor carriers:
- For‑hire carrier: transports others’ property for compensation (requires insurance, strict regulations).
- Private motor carrier: transports its own goods (or nothing at all) – “usually” as part of a business, but “usually” ≠ “always.” Your trust is the “not always” part.
🔥 Result: FMCSA determines: “Applicant will operate private passenger motor vehicle, non‑commercial only, non‑business, private property motor carrier. No insurance required.”

📝 Step 2 – FMCSA Application Key Fields

🔹 Applicant type: Trust (not a corporation). Title: Trustee.
🔹 Legal business name: Your trust name (e.g., “Demand Creators Ministry Trust”).
🔹 EIN or SSN: Use EIN if you have a trust; otherwise SSN (but expect spam).
🔹 Operation type:
  • Transport property? ✅ YES (your own property).
  • Compensation for transporting? ❌ NO (not for hire).
  • Transport your own property? ✅ YES.
  • Interstate commerce? ⚠️ You can say NO (intrastate only) to simplify.
  • Transport passengers for compensation? ❌ NO.
  • Transport passengers NOT for compensation? ✅ YES (friends/family).
🧠 Cargo types to select: Household goods, general freight, fresh produce, passengers, other. (All non‑hazardous.)
Non‑CMVs (non‑commercial motor vehicles): Enter the number of vehicles you own (e.g., 2).
Commercial motor vehicles: ZERO in every box.

✨ Step 3 – FMCSA Determination (The Golden Text)

🎉 After completing the maze, the FMCSA system will display:
“FMCSA has determined the applicant will operate private passenger motor vehicle, non‑commercial only, non‑business, private property motor carrier.”
Application fee: $0. Insurance required: NONE.

This determination is your “federal registration.” Print the company snapshot from the FMCSA website. This is the document you show police instead of state registration.

⚡ Note: If you choose “interstate,” you may need to refile after initial approval. For simplicity, choose “intrastate” (within state) – same legal effect for private use.

📍 Address Issue – How to Beat the PO Box Rejection

🔥 Problem: FMCSA system rejects PO boxes and many commercial mailboxes.
Solution (Brandon’s workaround): Use a real street address (e.g., a UPS Store or mailbox center) with a unit number, AND add “without the US per 28 USC 1746” as part of address line 2. Example:
“1234 Main Street, Unit B, without the United States per 28 USC 1746, Glendale CA 91206”
This signals you are not establishing residency.
