1. Jeffrey Epstein Background

Official Profile: Financier, convicted sex offender. Born 1953, died 2019. Managed billions for elite clients; owned private islands, planes, mansions.

Epstein rose from teacher to Wall Street banker (Bear Stearns), then money manager for billionaires like Les Wexner (Victoria's Secret). Convicted in 2008 Florida plea deal for soliciting underage prostitution (served 13 months with work release).

Re-arrested 2019 on federal sex trafficking charges. Died in jail (official: suicide; anomalies noted below).
