🏞️ Land Patents – Reclaim Your Property (Ron Gibson Method)

📜 Land patent = original title from the sovereign (US government) to the first owner. All subsequent deeds (quitclaim, grant deeds) are derivatives. If you trace your property’s chain of title back to the original land patent, you have superior title.

Step 1: Find your land patent. Go to the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) Land Catalog (glorecords.blm.gov). Search by state, county, township. Download the patent image (often signed by a president – e.g., FDR). Certified copy: ~$6.

Step 2: Build chain of title. From the patent down to your deed. Collect quitclaim deeds, grant deeds, probate records from county recorder. Create a timeline.

Step 3: File your claim. Record a “Notice of Claim of Interest” or “Affidavit of Chain of Title” with the county recorder. Include the patent, all deeds, and an affidavit asserting your superior title.

📄 Deed vs. Title – Simple Clarification

Title: ownership (abstract concept).
Deed: the physical document that transfers title.
Quitclaim deed: transfers whatever interest the grantor has, no warranties – used to clear clouds on title.

📬 Certified Mail, Green Cards & Evidence Chain

📮 Forms you need (all FREE from USPS):
- PS Form 3800: Certified Mail receipt (has tracking number).
- PS Form 3811: Return Receipt (green card) – the recipient signs it; it’s mailed back to you as proof.
- Priority Mail envelopes: free at post office.
- Registered Mail (red sticker): for irreplaceable items (original titles, gold coins, wet‑ink endorsements). More secure, slower, more expensive.
✅ Procedure:
1. Fill out PS 3800 with your return address and recipient’s address.
2. Attach 3800 to your envelope.
3. Fill out PS 3811 – check “Adult Signature” and “Certified Mail” boxes.
4. Affix 3811 to the back of the envelope.
5. Hand to post office clerk. They will stamp, process, and give you a receipt.
6. When delivered, the green card (3811) comes back to you with signature & date.
7. Staple the green card to a copy of the document you sent. This is your evidence for court.

⚠️ Don’t try to mail for free by writing things on the envelope – it works 50% of the time, angers your local post office, and you lose evidence. “The relationship with your post office is priceless. Send them edible arrangements.”
