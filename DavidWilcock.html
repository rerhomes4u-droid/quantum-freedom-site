A Voice for Disclosure

Who David Wilcock was

David Wilcock was one of the most prominent voices in the UFO disclosure movement, a New York Times bestselling author, and a regular contributor to the History Channel's "Ancient Aliens." He dedicated his life to investigating UFOs, ancient civilizations, consciousness science, and the possibility that humanity was on the verge of a major spiritual "ascension."

Born in Rotterdam, New York on March 8, 1973, Wilcock graduated from the State University of New York at New Paltz with a degree in psychology. He authored multiple bestselling books, including "The Source Field Investigations" and "The Synchronicity Key," and built a YouTube following of over 500,000 subscribers. He was 53 years old at the time of his death.

⚠️ The Circumstances

David Wilcock died on April 20, 2026, in Boulder County, Colorado, from a self-inflicted gunshot wound following a police response to a mental health crisis call. His death came just days after President Trump ordered an investigation into the mysterious deaths and disappearances of at least 14 scientists connected to nuclear, space, and UFO research programs. In his final livestream on April 19 — the day before his death — Wilcock said: "Every day that I have on this Earth is a gift and a blessing, and I'm very grateful for that, because, frankly, people are disappearing. Scientists are missing."

The Missing Scientists

The pattern Wilcock warned about

In the weeks before his death, David Wilcock repeatedly warned that scientists connected to sensitive government research programs were disappearing or dying under mysterious circumstances. At least 14 scientists, space engineers, and top officials connected to NASA, the military, and the Department of Energy have gone missing or died since 2021. The FBI, NASA, and the House Oversight Committee have launched investigations.

🚨 The Pattern Wilcock Warned About

"People are disappearing. Scientists are missing." — David Wilcock, April 19, 2026 (the day before his death)

👨‍🚀 Matthew James Sullivan

Air Force intelligence officer who worked on covert legacy UFO programs. Died at home in Falls Church, Virginia, on May 12, 2024, just months before he was set to testify before Congress about secret government UFO programs. Rep. Eric Burlison called his death of "grave concern" with "implications for national security."

🚀 Joshua LeBlanc

Former NASA engineer who worked on nuclear propulsion systems. Died in July 2025 when his Tesla smashed into a guardrail in Huntsville, Alabama. His car had been sitting at an airport for four hours before the crash. He left his phone and wallet at home.

⚛️ Dr. Ning Li

Physicist specializing in anti-gravity research. Hit by a car on the University of Alabama campus in 2014 and died from her injuries in July 2021. She founded AC Gravity and worked on gravity modification technology.

🔐 Steven Garcia

Government contractor with nuclear clearance at the Kansas City National Security Campus. Disappeared on August 28, 2025, after leaving his home in Albuquerque on foot with a handgun, leaving behind his phone, keys, and wallet.

🛸 Retired Air Force Maj. Gen. William Neil McCasland

Former commander who oversaw classified space weapons programs at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base. Disappeared on February 27, 2025, near his home in Albuquerque, New Mexico. His wife said he left on foot with a sidearm.

🌌 Monica Reza

Director of materials processing for NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Vanished in June 2025 while hiking in California. Her hiking companion said she was 30 feet behind and then simply disappeared. Her body was never recovered.

⭐ Michael David Hicks

NASA scientist who studied comets and asteroids. Died in July 2023. His death is part of the pattern being investigated.

🌙 Frank Maiwald

NASA researcher who worked on microwave radiometry and Earth science missions. Died in July 2024.

🔭 Carl Grillmair

Astrophysicist at Caltech who collaborated with NASA. Died in February 2026.

⚡ Nuno Loureiro

MIT physicist. Died in December 2025.

💫 Amy Eskridge

Founded the Institute for Exotic Science. Died in June 2022.

🏗️ Anthony Chavez

Worked for decades at Los Alamos National Laboratory as a construction foreman. Disappeared in May 2025 at age 78 after leaving his home on foot, leaving behind his wallet and keys.

📋 Melissa Casias

Administrative assistant at Los Alamos National Laboratory. Vanished without trace on June 26, 2025.

🔬 Jason Thomas

Worked at Novartis. Disappeared in December 2025; found dead in March 2026.
