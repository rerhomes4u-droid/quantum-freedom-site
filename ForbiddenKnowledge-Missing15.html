05The Sanctuary Host Vetting Protocol

The host is the most vulnerable point in the chain. The vetting is the protection.

Host Vetting Checklist
  • PMA membership in good standing. The host must be a member for at least 90 days.
  • Background check. Criminal record check in Colombia and country of residence.
  • Home inspection. The room must be safe, clean, and lockable. The home must have working utilities.
  • Interview. The host is interviewed by the Sanctuary Coordinator. The interview covers motivation, expectations, and ability to host.
  • Reference checks. Two references from PMA members who know the host.
  • Signed host agreement. The agreement from the previous document is signed.
  • Insurance confirmation. The host confirms homeowner's or renter's insurance covers guests.
  • Registration with the attorney. The host is registered with the Colombian attorney.
  • Stipend setup. The host's bank account is set up for the monthly stipend.
  • Emergency contact. The host provides a 24/7 emergency contact.
