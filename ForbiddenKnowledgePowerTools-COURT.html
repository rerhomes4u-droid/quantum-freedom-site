Article 1 vs Article 3 Courts

You are standing in an Article 1 Court. Article 1 Courts don't have the authority to prosecute criminal law, yet they do it every day.

The Constitution only grants two jurisdictions for criminal cases: Common Law or Admiralty. These must be handled in Article 3 courts.
