03The Reporting Protocol: If You See Something, Report It

The Claim

📢 Report It

If you find evidence of child exploitation, trafficking, or abuse, here is the protocol. Do not investigate alone. Do not confront. Report.

1

NCMEC CyberTipline. The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. Report online at report.cybertip.org or call 1-800-843-5678. Available 24/7. missingkids.org [citation:5][citation:12]

2

FBI Tips. Submit a tip online at tips.fbi.gov or call 1-800-CALL-FBI. For emergencies, call 911. [citation:5][citation:12]

3

Simply Report App. A free mobile app launched in 2025. Anonymous real-time reporting. Connects survivors to Safe House Project's network of safe houses and certified care providers. Available on Apple App Store and Google Play. safehouseproject.org [citation:11]

4

Document everything. Take screenshots. Save URLs. Note dates and times. Do not alter the evidence. Do not share it publicly. Report it to the authorities and to NCMEC.
