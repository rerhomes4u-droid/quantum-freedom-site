01ESAL Registration Checklist — Colombia

This is the step-by-step checklist for the attorney and the trustees. The ESAL is the legal vehicle. The trust is the accountability mechanism. The public ledger is the transparency mechanism.

Step 1 — Name Verification
  • Verify the name availability through the RUES portal (ruesfront.rues.org.co) [citation:10].
  • The name must not conflict with an existing ESAL or commercial entity.
Step 2 — Acta de Constitución
  • Prepare the acta de constitución, indicating the will to constitute the ESAL, the approval of the statutes, the duration, and the appointment of the junta directiva [citation:10].
  • Include the list of associates: full names, cédula numbers, and domicile [citation:4].
  • The acta must be recognized by the president and secretary of the meeting before a notary, or by personal presentation before a Cámara de Comercio official [citation:10].
Step 3 — Statutes
  • Prepare the statutes in accordance with Article 40 of Decreto 2150 de 1995 [citation:10].
  • The statutes must define: the object social (the rescue fund), the governance structure, the fiscal obligations, and the dissolution clause.
Step 4 — Acceptance Letters
  • Obtain the written acceptance of every appointed officer (president, secretary, treasurer, fiscal auditor) [citation:4][citation:10].
  • Each letter must include the officer's name, cédula number, and the date of acceptance.
Step 5 — RUES Registration
  • Complete the Formulario Único Empresarial y Social (RUES) [citation:10].
  • The form must be signed by the legal representative, without erasures or amendments.
  • Present the form at the Cámara de Comercio, either virtually or in person [citation:10].
Step 6 — Payment of Fees
  • Pay the registration fees at the Cámara de Comercio [citation:4].
  • Pay the impuesto de registro at the Gobernación [citation:10].
  • Keep the receipts. The public ledger will record these expenses.
Step 7 — RUT (DIAN)
  • Register the ESAL with the DIAN to obtain the RUT [citation:5].
  • The RUT is required to open a bank account and to receive donations lawfully.
Step 8 — The Régimen Tributario Especial Decision
  • Option A: Simple ESAL. The fund receives donations, but donations are not tax-deductible for the donor. The ESAL is not in the RTE. This is the faster path.
  • Option B: ESAL in the RTE. The fund qualifies for the Régimen Tributario Especial through the DIAN. Donations become tax-deductible for the donor. This requires the calificación process, annual updates, and compliance with the RTE requirements [citation:5][citation:11].
  • The trustees must decide which path. The public page must state which path was chosen.
Step 9 — Bank Account
  • Open a Colombian bank account in the name of the ESAL.
  • Required documents: RUT, certificate of existence and legal representation, cédula of the legal representative.
  • The bank will require KYC and due diligence. The fund must be prepared to explain the source of funds and the destination of funds.

02Compliance Policy

This is the policy that governs how the fund handles donations, records expenditures, reports to the public ledger, and stays lawful under Colombian tax rules.

Article 1 — Donation Intake

1.1. All donations are received into the ESAL's Colombian bank account.

1.2. Every donation is recorded with the donor's name (or "anonymous" if the donor requests), the amount, the date, and the designated purpose (Extraction, Sanctuary, or Witness).

1.3. If the ESAL is in the RTE, the fund issues a tax certificate to the donor. If the ESAL is not in the RTE, the fund issues a receipt that states the donation is not tax-deductible.

Article 2 — Expenditure Recording

2.1. Every expenditure is recorded with the date, the amount, the payee, the purpose (Extraction, Sanctuary, or Witness), and the supporting document (invoice, receipt, or contract).

2.2. Expenditures above $500 USD require the approval of two trustees.

2.3. The attorney's fees, the host's stipend, and the transport costs are recorded separately for each extraction.

Article 3 — Public Ledger Reporting

3.1. The public ledger is updated every Sunday by the Witness Lead.

3.2. The ledger shows: total raised, total spent, current balance, extractions funded, extractions completed, sanctuary hosts committed, and Witness Network members active.

3.3. The ledger does not show: the names of the rescued, the names of the hosts, the routes, the attorneys, or the code words.

3.4. Any member may request a line-by-line audit of any expenditure. The audit is conducted by two trustees who were not involved in the expenditure.

Article 4 — Tax Compliance

4.1. The ESAL files its annual tax return with the DIAN, whether or not it is in the RTE.

4.2. If the ESAL is in the RTE, it must comply with the annual update requirements, the memoria económica (if revenues exceed 160,000 UVT), and the transparency platform reporting [citation:11].

4.3. Failure to comply with the RTE requirements results in the ESAL being treated as a commercial entity under the general tax regime [citation:11].

Article 5 — Confidentiality

5.1. The rescued individuals' identities, locations, and stories are confidential. They are never published.

5.2. The hosts' identities and addresses are confidential. They are never published.

5.3. The attorneys' identities and the code words are confidential. They are never written in any digital system.

WARNING: The fund does not accept donations from sources that would compromise its independence or expose it to legal risk. The trustees may reject any donation for any reason. The fund does not accept cash donations above $1,000 USD. All donations above that amount must be by bank transfer.

The Legal Requirements — Colombia

The legal path depends on the beneficiary's situation. The attorney determines which path applies. The fund pays for the path. The beneficiary must meet the requirements of the path.

Path A — Refugee Status

Eligibility: The person has a well-founded fear of persecution for race, religion, nationality, membership in a particular social group, or political opinion; or their life, security, or freedom is threatened by generalized violence, foreign aggression, internal conflicts, or massive human rights violations [citation:1][citation:7].

Requirement: The person must be inside Colombian territory to apply [citation:1].

Process: Form DP-FO-273, sent to `solicitudesentramite@cancilleria.gov.co` [citation:7][citation:13].

Interim status: The salvoconducto SC-2 allows the person to remain lawfully, access health and education, and work while the application is pending [citation:7].

Path B — Visa M Refugiado

Eligibility: The person has been recognized as a refugee by the Colombian state [citation:19].

Benefit: The visa grants an open work permit and allows the holder to accumulate time toward residency after 5 years [citation:19].

Path C — Other Visa Categories

If the person does not qualify for refugee status, the attorney may identify other lawful paths: Visa M Pensionado, Visa M Inversionista, Visa M Profesional Independiente, or others [citation:14]. The fund does not force a path. It identifies the lawful path and supports it.
