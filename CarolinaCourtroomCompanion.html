SPECIAL APPEARANCE • RESERVING ALL RIGHTS • SEEKING EQUITY
1 Opening Declaration

Before anything else, you must establish your special appearance. Stand up, speak clearly and slowly:

"Your Honor, I appear before you specially, under duress, threat, and coercion, due to the unlawful seizure and detention of my son, Ethan Bernal Herrera. I do not submit to the jurisdiction of this statutory court for any matter other than the immediate return of my child and the correction of this grievous wrong. I make this special appearance reserving all my rights, and I seek equity, as this court sits as a court of conscience."
✨ Take a breath. You are establishing your sovereign position. This is your truth.
2 Question on Jurisdiction & Natural Bond

After your opening, ask this foundational question about the court's authority:

"By what lawful authority, in equity and good conscience, did any agent of the state sever the sacred, natural bond between a breastfeeding mother and her infant son, a child who is flesh of my flesh and a free-born American citizen? I demand to see the affidavit of specific, imminent harm that justified this act of violence against our natural relationship."
💡 Remember: You are asking them to justify the unjustifiable. Hold your ground and wait for an answer.
3 Exposing the Weaponization of the System

Now expose the pattern and the father's manipulation:

"The man who inflicted this injury upon me in 2020—a fact documented by your own sheriff's office—has now learned to inflict a greater injury using your court as his weapon. Where in equity does this court find the power to reward a documented abuser and punish his victim with the theft of her child? Is this court a shield for the vulnerable, or a sword for the manipulator?"

4 Challenging the Court's Failure of Duty

Ask about their failure to review the evidence:

"Before granting an order that would cause irreparable harm, did this court, in its equitable duty, review the 2020 incident report naming me as the victim? If not, why was this foundational evidence ignored? If so, how in conscience did you decide that the accuser in a new case was more credible than the documented victim in the old one? I require an answer."
✨ You are holding the court accountable. This is your right as a mother seeking equity.
5 Demanding the Equitable Remedy

Now state the solution clearly and powerfully:

"Equity acts to undo a wrong. The wrong is the unlawful separation. The only just remedy is immediate reunification. Will this court now issue the writ of restitution that returns my son to my arms, his rightful place, and corrects this manifest injustice before further harm is done?"
💡 This is the core of your request. Say it with conviction. You are not asking for a favor—you are demanding justice.
6 Final Appeal to Conscience

End with this powerful appeal to the judge's conscience:

"I put this to the court's conscience: If the roles were reversed—if I, the documented victim with the black eye, had called and accused him—would this court have taken his child from him? We both know the answer. That is proof of bias and a failure of equity. I demand this court look into its own conscience and reverse this order."
