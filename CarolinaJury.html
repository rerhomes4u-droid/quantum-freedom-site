Jury Nullification Strategy

This is our most powerful tool. We will educate the jury about their power without directly violating court rules.

What is Jury Nullification?

Jurors have the constitutional power to acquit a defendant even if they believe the person technically broke the law, if they believe the law is being unjustly applied or the prosecution is wrong.

We cannot directly tell jurors about this power, but we can ask questions that make them discover it themselves.

During Jury Selection (Voir Dire):

  • "Did you know that as a juror, you have the power to make a real difference in this type of case?"
  • "Are you aware that sometimes laws are applied in ways that can be unjust to innocent people?"
  • "Do you believe you have a responsibility to consider not just the letter of the law, but also how it's being applied in this specific situation?"
  • "Would you be able to deliver a not guilty verdict if you believed the prosecution was using loopholes to target an innocent person?"

During Trial (When questioning witnesses):

  • "Are you aware that this type of case often involves exploitation of legal loopholes?"
  • "Would it surprise you to learn that there's a pattern of this specific legal mechanism being used against innocent individuals?"

Pattern of Behavior Evidence:

We will present arrest records showing how undocumented aliens have used these loopholes to exploit the system. We'll show:

  1. Multiple similar cases with the same pattern
  2. How the system is being manipulated
  3. That you are collateral damage in this exploitation

Closing Argument Theme:

"Ladies and gentlemen of the jury, you're here because you have the power to make a real difference. You have the power to say 'no' to a system being exploited. You have the power to protect an innocent nursing mother from becoming another victim of these loopholes. With that power comes responsibility - the responsibility to deliver true justice, not just a technical verdict."

Your Constitutional Rights

You have powerful rights that the system hopes you don't know about.

Right to a Speedy Trial

The Constitution guarantees you a timely trial. Each postponement without cause violates this right. We will document every delay and file motions accordingly.

Right to Confront Witnesses

You can question anyone who testifies against you. We will challenge the credibility of any witness exploiting the system.

Right to Due Process

This means fair procedures. Being ignored, rushed, or given the "run around" violates due process. We will object each time.

Right to Remain Silent

You cannot be forced to testify against yourself. We will carefully decide if you should take the stand.

When Rights Are Violated:

If the judge or prosecutor ignores your rights, we will say:

"Your Honor, we object on constitutional grounds. My client's Sixth Amendment right to a speedy trial and Fourteenth Amendment right to due process are being violated by these continued delays."

Motion to Dismiss

If delays continue, we will file a motion to dismiss based on:

  1. Violation of speedy trial rights
  2. Prosecutorial misconduct in using delay tactics
  3. Selective prosecution (targeting you while ignoring actual criminals)
