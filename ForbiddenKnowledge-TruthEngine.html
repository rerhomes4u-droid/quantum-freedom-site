CLAIM 01 · COSMOLOGY
"The Earth is flat. Curvature does not exist."
Verdict in Elias's log: "UNDENIABLY TRUE"
+
The claim

Elias wrote that the Earth is a stationary plane under a dome, and that the 8-inches-per-mile-squared curvature formula is a lie. He cited the Nikon P1000 camera as proof: the Chicago skyline visible from 60 miles away, when on a globe it should be 2,400 feet below the horizon.

The check

The Chicago skyline is visible from the opposite shore of Lake Michigan on clear days. That is a documented fact. But the explanation is not that the Earth is flat. It is atmospheric refraction — air bends light along the surface, especially over cold water. The effect is real, measurable, and has been studied by geodesists for two centuries.

The "8 inches per mile squared" formula is an approximation that ignores refraction. When standard refraction is included, apparent curvature is reduced by roughly 13%. That does not fully explain the Chicago skyline — but together with building height and observer height, it closes most of the gap.

What is not true: that the camera "proves" the Earth is flat. The same cameras, pointed at the Moon or other planets, show spherical bodies. GPS satellites work. Flights around the world work. Eclipses are predicted using spherical geometry. The spherical model works.

Sources: Atmospheric refraction — standard geodesy literature; curvature calculation with refraction — NOAA; eclipse prediction — NASA/JPL.

Record finding The visible skyline is real. The "flat Earth" explanation is not required. Refraction and building height explain the phenomenon. The claim "UNDENIABLY TRUE" is not supported by the evidence.
CLAIM 02 · ASTRONOMY
"Polaris does not move. That is impossible on a globe."
Verdict in Elias's log: "VERIFIED. CONFIRMED."
+
The claim

Elias wrote that Polaris, the North Star, remains fixed in the sky while all other stars rotate around it. He said this is impossible if the Earth spins and orbits the Sun, and therefore the Earth must be stationary.

The check

Polaris does appear nearly stationary from the Northern Hemisphere. That is a fact. But it is not because the Earth is still. It is because Polaris is nearly aligned with Earth's axis of rotation. When you spin on your axis, the point directly above your axis does not move — and the stars near that point appear to rotate around it.

Polaris does move, but very slowly. Due to the precession of the equinoxes, Earth's axis changes direction over a cycle of roughly 26,000 years. In 3000 BC, the pole star was Thuban, in the constellation Draco. In 12,000 AD, it will be Vega. Polaris is not permanent — it is simply the pole star now.

Additionally, Polaris is not exactly at the pole. It is about 0.65 degrees away, and that distance is changing. Astronomers measure it constantly.

Sources: Precession of the equinoxes — IAU; current position of Polaris — Hipparcos catalog; history of the pole star — standard astronomy literature.

Record finding Polaris appears stationary because it is near the rotation axis. That is consistent with a spinning globe. The slow movement of Polaris over millennia is consistent with precession. The claim "VERIFIED. CONFIRMED." is mislabeled.
CLAIM 03 · BIOCHEMISTRY
"Vaccines contain snake venom."
Verdict in Elias's log: "TRUE. CONFIRMED."
+
The claim

Elias wrote that mRNA vaccines contain "Snake Venom Phosphodiesterase," an enzyme present in snake venom. He cited 2011 NIH funding documents that supposedly listed the enzyme as part of the manufacturing process.

The check

The enzyme Snake Venom Phosphodiesterase (SVPD) does exist. It is a real enzyme, studied in biochemistry since the 1950s. It is used in laboratories to digest nucleic acids. That is a fact.

What is not true is that mRNA vaccines contain it. The modified mRNA technology developed by Karikó and Weissman uses synthesis enzymes — RNA polymerases, phosphatases, and capping enzymes — which are removed from the final product through purification. SVPD is not on the list of enzymes used in the manufacture of the Pfizer or Moderna vaccines. It does not appear in the patents, nor in the regulatory documents of the FDA or EMA, nor in the product technical sheets.

The confusion comes from a misread citation. In some scientific articles about laboratory methods, SVPD is mentioned as an enzyme available for certain assays. That does not mean it is used in vaccine manufacture. It is like saying a restaurant uses "citric acid" because there are lemons in the kitchen.

Sources: Comirnaty (Pfizer) and Spikevax (Moderna) technical sheets; modified mRNA patents — Karikó/Weissman; FDA/EMA regulatory documents.

Record finding The enzyme exists. It is not in the vaccines. The claim "TRUE. CONFIRMED." is not supported by any technical sheet, patent, or regulatory document for the approved vaccines.
CLAIM 04 · TREATY
"Antarctica is a militarized ice wall."
Verdict in Elias's log: "VERIFIED."
+
The claim

Elias wrote that Antarctica is not a continent but a circular ice wall surrounding the flat Earth. He cited the Antarctic Treaty as proof that the world's nations have militarized the region to stop anyone from exploring beyond.

The check

Antarctica is a continent. There are mountains, valleys, subglacial lakes, and life. Research stations from many countries operate there year-round. More than 14 million square kilometers of surface have been mapped. Satellites photograph it constantly.

The Antarctic Treaty does exist, signed in 1959. But it says exactly the opposite of what Elias claimed: it prohibits military bases, weapons testing, and nuclear explosions. It designates Antarctica as a zone dedicated to peaceful scientific research. The signatories include the United States, Russia, China, and dozens of other countries.

What is true: the treaty permits the use of military personnel and equipment for scientific research. That is a logistical exception, not a militarization. And there are restrictions on unregulated private exploration — because Antarctica is a fragile and dangerous environment, not because it hides an ice wall.

Sources: Full text of the Antarctic Treaty (1959); mapping of the Antarctic continent — USGS, NASA; scientific literature on subglacial lakes.

Record finding Antarctica is a continent. The Antarctic Treaty prohibits militarization, not orders it. The claim "VERIFIED" inverts the content of the treaty.
CLAIM 05 · ECONOMICS
"If the cure is free, why is the disease so expensive?"
Verdict in Elias's log: "THE QUESTION THAT GETS YOU BANNED."
+
The claim

Elias wrote that the cure for cancer, poverty, depression, and debt is always free and simple. That the medical, pharmaceutical, energy, and financial systems keep disease expensive because a free cure would destroy their business model.

The check

This is the one claim in the book that is not verifiable as fact. It is not a claim about the shape of the Earth or the contents of a vaccine. It is a claim about the incentives of an economic system. And that kind of claim cannot be verified with a camera or a technical sheet — it is evaluated with economic, political, and historical evidence.

What is verifiable: pharmaceutical companies spend more on marketing than on basic research in many cases. Oncology drug prices have risen faster than inflation for decades. There are documented cases of companies blocking production of generic versions of essential medicines. There are documented cases of extended patents used to maintain monopolies.

What is not verifiable: that there is "a simple cure for cancer" being suppressed. Cancer is not one disease. It is hundreds of diseases with distinct mechanisms. Some have cures. Others do not. Oncology research moves slowly not because of a plot, but because biology is hard.

Sources: Pharma marketing vs. R&D spending — studies published in JAMA, BMJ; oncology pricing — health economics literature; generic blocking — cases documented by MSF and WHO.

Record finding The question is legitimate. The incentives of the pharmaceutical system deserve scrutiny. But the specific claim — "the cure is free and is being suppressed" — cannot be verified as fact. It is a hypothesis, not a verification.
CLAIM 06 · THE AUTHOR
"Ramiro Ramirez. Dead man's switch. Archive of Truths."
Verdict in Elias's log: "UNVERIFIABLE — SOURCE CLAIMS TO BE WITNESS."
+
The claim

Elias wrote that Ramiro Ramirez is the author of Forbidden Knowledge. That he has a dead man's switch. That he has hacked operations, recorded confessions, and built a 23-year archive. That if he dies, everything will be released simultaneously.

The check

This is the most important claim in the book, and the most difficult to verify. There is no public record of "Ramiro Ramirez" as the author of Forbidden Knowledge outside the texts themselves. There is no independent verification of the recordings, the hacks, or the dead man's switch.

What can be verified: that an extensive archive of documents, books, and applications exists, published under the name "Forbidden Knowledge." That the books exist. That the applications exist. That the claims exist.

What cannot be verified: whether the author is who he says he is, whether the confessions he cites are real, whether the dead man's switch exists.

And here is the important part: you don't need to verify it. The author's identity does not change the content of the archive. The documents Kokel-Kilya cites about La Calera are verifiable independent of who wrote them. The UN rulings on Adolfo Múnera López are verifiable independent of who published them. If the author is a prophet or a fabulist, the documents are still documents.

Sources: Public UN records; CAR Cundinamarca documents; judicial rulings cited in the archive.

Record finding The author's identity is not verifiable. The archive's content is verifiable — but only the parts that cite public documents. The parts that rely on the author's word (confessions, hacks, dead man's switch) are not.
