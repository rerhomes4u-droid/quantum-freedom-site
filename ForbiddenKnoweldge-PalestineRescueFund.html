05How We Do This Lawfully

The Legal Architecture

The PMA. The Amnesty Coalition for Unity is a private membership association. The fund sits inside the PMA. The donors are members or invited guests. The fund is not a public charity soliciting the general public.

The Trust. The fund is held in trust. The trustees are fiduciaries. The accounting is separate. The public ledger is the trust's reporting mechanism.

The Attorney. A Colombian attorney on retainer handles every visa, every document, every interaction with immigration. The fund does not smuggle. The fund does not break laws. The fund pays for lawful entry, lawful residence, and lawful integration.

The Hosts. Every host is a PMA member. Every host is vetted. Every host is registered with the attorney. Every host's identity is known to the fund and not public.

06What the Fund Does Not Do

The fund does not smuggle. Every extraction is lawful. Every entry is documented. Every visa is filed through the attorney. If the lawful path is not available, the extraction is delayed, not abandoned.
The fund does not publish the names. The rescued are never named. The hosts are never named. The routes are never named. The attorneys are never named. The fund publishes the totals and the numbers. It does not publish the people.
The fund does not promise outcomes. The fund promises to try. The fund promises to be transparent. The fund promises to account for every dollar. The fund does not promise that every extraction will succeed. It promises that every extraction will be lawful, documented, and honest.
