The Covenant of Custody

Five articles. Memorized, not written. Enforced by the cell, not by punishment.

What this body politic is. It is not a government. It is not a court. It is not a lawyer. It is a network of cells who hold testimony in many hands, so that it is harder to erase. It does not promise that anyone will be punished. It does not promise that anyone will be protected. It promises only that testimony will be kept, and that the cost paid will be remembered.
  1. I will hold what is given to me, and I will not alter it.
  2. I will tell the witness plainly what the Book can and cannot do.
  3. I will hold no complete copy, and I will know who holds the rest.
  4. I will train at least one successor, and I will not let the cell die with me.
  5. I will not use the Book for any purpose other than its keeping.

The Three Tiers

Witnesses
Those who record their testimony. The reason the body exists. They may come once and never return. They may stay and become carriers.
Custodians
Those who hold the Book. Organized in cells of three to seven. No cell holds a complete copy. Each cell knows which other cells hold what it does not.
Council
Five to nine people who hold the map: which cells hold what, how cells communicate, how the body is reconstituted after loss. The Council does not hold testimony. It holds the memory of the structure.

The Measure of Success

The body succeeds if, after the loss of any single custodian, any single cell, or any single public face, the Book can still be reconstituted and the witness's testimony can still be found.

That is the only measure. Not growth. Not influence. Not recognition. Reconstitution after loss.

The IACHR Route

Any person or organization can file a precautionary measure request with the Inter-American Commission on Human Rights. It is not fast enough for immediate danger. It is a record that makes disappearance more costly.

  • Who can file: anyone, not just the victim.
  • What to include: name, risk, the state's failure to protect.
  • Where: the IACHR portal.
  • The limit: the state will know the beneficiary's identity if a measure is granted.

What This Body Politic Does Not Claim

  • It is not a sovereign nation.
  • It does not create legal immunity for any witness.
  • It cannot compel any authority to act on any testimony.
  • It cannot guarantee the safety of any witness.
  • It cannot prevent its own copies from being seized, subpoenaed, or destroyed.

What it does claim: that a testimony recorded, corroborated where possible, held in many hands, and preserved with its chain of custody intact is harder to erase than a testimony spoken once and forgotten. That the record of what was paid is itself a form of justice, even when no court acts. That the future has a right to know what was said, by whom, and at what cost.
