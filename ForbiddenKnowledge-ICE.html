▣ KNOW YOUR RIGHTS
Stay calm. Do not run. Do not lie. Do not open the door without a judge-signed warrant.

▣ THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER

An ICE warrant is not a judicial warrant.

"Slide it under the door."

If it is not signed by a judge, you do not have to open the door.

🚪 ICE IS AT YOUR DOOR

STEP 1
Do not open the door. Opening the door does not give them permission to enter, but it is safer to speak through the door.
STEP 2
Ask: "Are you immigration agents? What are you here for?" Ask them to show a badge or ID through the window or peephole.
STEP 3
Ask: "Do you have a warrant signed by a judge?" If they say yes, ask them to slide it under the door or hold it up to the window.
CRITICAL
An ICE administrative warrant (Form I-200, I-205) is NOT a judicial warrant. It does not give them the right to enter your home. Only a warrant signed by a judge does.
"I do not consent to your entry. I am exercising my right to remain silent. I wish to speak with a lawyer."
DO NOT: Open the door. Sign anything. Lie. Resist if they force entry. Say anything about your status or birthplace.
IF THEY FORCE ENTRY
Do not resist. State clearly: "I do not consent to this entry or search. I am exercising my right to remain silent. I want a lawyer." Then stay silent.

🚶 ICE STOPPED YOU OUTSIDE

STEP 1
Stay calm. Do not run. Do not argue. Keep your hands visible.
STEP 2
Ask: "Am I free to go?"
IF YES
You can calmly and silently walk away. Do not answer questions.
IF NO
You are being detained. Say: "I want to remain silent. I want to speak to a lawyer."
DO NOT
Answer questions about where you were born, your citizenship, or how you entered the country. Do not consent to a search.
"I do not consent to a search. I am exercising my right to remain silent."

🚗 ICE PULLED YOU OVER

STEP 1
Pull over safely. Turn off the engine. Turn on the interior light. Keep your hands on the wheel.
STEP 2
Show your driver's license, registration, and proof of insurance if requested.
STEP 3
You do not have to answer questions about your immigration status. Say: "I remain silent."
DO NOT: Consent to a search of your car. Do not lie. Do not provide false documents.

🏢 ICE IS AT YOUR WORK

PUBLIC AREA
If you are in a public area (lobby, parking lot, dining area), ICE may approach you. You still have the right to remain silent.
PRIVATE AREA
If ICE wants to enter a private area (kitchen, office, storage), they need either your consent or a judicial warrant. You can say no.
DOCUMENTS
ICE may have a subpoena for records. A subpoena is not a warrant. You have the right to contest it in court.
"I do not consent to a search of this area. Please show me a judicial warrant."

🔒 YOU ARE DETAINED

STEP 1
Say clearly: "I want to remain silent. I want to speak to a lawyer."
STEP 2
Do not sign anything. Do not answer questions about your status. Do not sign a "Stipulated Removal Order" or agree to "voluntary departure" without a lawyer.
STEP 3
Ask for a list of free or low-cost lawyers. The government does not have to provide one, but you have the right to hire one and to have one present at hearings.
YOUR RIGHTS
You have the right to contact your consulate. You have the right to a bond hearing before an immigration judge. You have the right to have your attorney with you at any hearing.
REMEMBER: Your 'A' number. Give it to your family. They can use ICE's detainee locator to find you. Keep a copy of your immigration documents with someone you trust.

🛠 SPECIAL TOOLS

JUDICIAL WARRANT
SIGNED BY A JUDGE
You MUST comply
ICE ADMINISTRATIVE WARRANT
SIGNED BY ICE
You do NOT have to comply
LOOK FOR
A judge's signature. A court seal. The words 'United States District Court.' If it says 'Department of Homeland Security' or 'ICE,' it is NOT a judicial warrant.
