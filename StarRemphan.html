THE STAR OF REMPHAN

The Cult That Never Died · The Sacrifice That Still Smokes

Remphan (Ῥαιφάν). A name spoken only once in Scripture — by a dying man, Stephen, as the stones began to fly: "Ye took up the tabernacle of Moloch, and the star of your god Remphan, figures which ye made to worship them: and I will carry you away beyond Babylon" (Acts 7:43).

Remphan is not a footnote. He is Saturn. Chiun. Kiyyun. The baleful star that ancient astrologers feared above all others. His worship demanded the fire, the drum, the burning hands of Molech. What follows is a biblical and historical exposition of the gods that seduced Israel and still whisper to the empires of this age.

📖 Amos 5:26-27 — "But ye have borne the tabernacle of your Moloch and Chiun your images, the star of your god, which ye made to yourselves. Therefore will I cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the Lord, whose name is The God of Hosts."

🔥 MOLECH · THE KING OF THE FURNACE

Molech — Milcom, Malik, Kronos. His name means "King," but his throne was a furnace. A hollow brass effigy, heated red, arms outstretched to receive the offering. The drums beat to drown the screams. Plutarch and Diodorus Siculus recorded Carthaginians burning their most beloved children to Saturn during national crises. The Tophet (place of burning) has yielded urns of calcined infant bones — firstborn sons, offered in exchange for fortune.

Sacrifice categories (Phoenician inscriptions): Molk-Baal (firstborn son, burned alive), Molk-Adom (substitute lamb), Molk-’omer (grain with blood), Molk-shekhem (community leaders' children).

⚖️ 2 Kings 3:27 — Mesha, King of Moab, took his eldest son and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. "And there came great wrath against Israel."

🌩️ BAAL · THE RIDER OF THE CLOUDS

Baal (Hadad, Bel, Melqart) — storm god, fertility lord, the golden calf of Sinai made full. His worship was fire, blood, and sacred prostitution (hieros gamos).

  • Autumn: Women wept for Tammuz (Ezekiel 8:14). Cakes baked for the Queen of Heaven (Jeremiah 44).
  • Spring: Priests slashed themselves (1 Kings 18), offering a white bull — Baal's animal.
  • Summer: Children passed through fire to Molech (Baal as Devourer).
🔥 1 Kings 18 — Elijah on Carmel: "How long halt ye between two opinions? If the Lord be God, follow him: but if Baal, then follow him." The people answered not a word.

🗡️ CHEMOSH · THE GOD OF MOAB

Chemosh received sacrifices on high places (bamot). Solomon built a high place for Chemosh on the Mount of Olives (1 Kings 11). The Mesha Stele (9th century BC) records: "I took the vessels of Yahweh and presented them before Chemosh." War captives, firstborn sons, and oaths of blood.

📜 Numbers 21:29 — "Woe to thee, Moab! thou art undone, O people of Chemosh: he hath given his sons that escaped, and his daughters, into captivity."

⭐ ASHTORETH · THE QUEEN OF HEAVEN

Ashtoreth (Astarte, Ishtar, Venus) — consort of Baal, goddess of fertility, war, and the evening star. Israelite women burned incense, baked cakes "marked with her image," and offered doves, menstrual blood libations, and — in Phoenician colonies — the blood of a firstborn daughter.

🍰 Jeremiah 44:17-19 — "We will burn incense to the queen of heaven... for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well."

🐟 DAGON · THE FISH-FATHER

Dagon, the Philistine god, half-man half-fish, master of grain and sea. His statue fell before the Ark of the Covenant (1 Samuel 5), broken on the threshold. Sacrifices: first sheaf of grain, fish burned on uncut stone, and the strange guilt offering of five golden tumors and five golden rats — the plague of hemorrhoids sent as judgment.

📦 1 Samuel 5:4 — "Behold, Dagon was fallen upon his face to the ground before the ark of the LORD; and the head of Dagon and both the palms of his hands were cut off."

🐐 BAPHOMET · THE GOAT OF MENDES

Baphomet — a composite figure popularized by Éliphas Lévi (19th c.), blending the goat of Mendes (Banebdjedet), the scapegoat Azazel (Leviticus 16), and Templar legends. The name likely derives from "Mahomet" (Muhammad), a Crusader-era corruption. The Goat of Mendes was a living sacred goat worshipped in Egypt with fertility rites. On Yom Kippur, one goat was sacrificed to Yahweh, the other (Azazel) sent into the wilderness bearing sins — later traditions associated Azazel with a desert demon.

🏜️ Leviticus 16:21-22 — "And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities... and send him away by the hand of a fit man into the wilderness."

🌾 TAMMUZ · THE WEEPING GOD

Dumuzid, Adonis — the dying and rising god, consort of Ishtar. Women wept for Tammuz at the north gate of the temple (Ezekiel 8:14). Grain offerings, incense, and ritual mourning marked his death each summer. His resurrection signaled the autumn rains.

These are not myths. They are the gods that consumed Canaan, Carthage, and Israel when she played the harlot. The prophets did not mince words. Neither should we.

⚡ THE INFECTION CONTINUES · A SOBER WARNING

The prophets spoke to their age — and to every age that bows to the star instead of the Creator. The sacrifice of children, the worship of fertility, the exaltation of power as god: these temptations never die. They simply change their masks.

But Scripture gives a clear dividing line: "Choose this day whom you will serve... but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD" (Joshua 24:15). The Star of Remphan still rises wherever innocent blood is shed in the name of policy, convenience, or greed. The call of Amos echoes: "Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream" (Amos 5:24).

⚠️ The judgment is not against any nation or people — but against the spiritual system that demands human sacrifice. The King who broke the power of Moloch is the same King who rose from the dead. His name is Jesus.

Remphan's star still shines over the throne of every empire that spills innocent blood and calls it policy. But you have heard the voice of the prophets. You have seen the brass hands of Molech. Now you must answer.

📜 Leviticus 18:21 — "And thou shalt not let any of thy seed pass through the fire to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God."

📜 Deuteronomy 12:31 — "Thou shalt not do so unto the LORD thy God: for every abomination to the LORD, which he hateth, have they done unto their gods; for even their sons and their daughters they have burnt in the fire to their gods."

📜 Psalm 106:37-38 — "Yea, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto devils, and shed innocent blood."

📜 Acts 7:42-43 — Stephen: "God turned, and gave them up to worship the host of heaven; as it is written in the book of the prophets, O ye house of Israel, have ye offered to me slain beasts and sacrifices... ye took up the tabernacle of Moloch, and the star of your god Remphan."
