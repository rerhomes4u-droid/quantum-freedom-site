📖 THE THREE ROLES — Which One Are You?

In every commercial relationship, there are three positions. The sovereignty space got this right. But they offered the wrong solution.

🔴 TRUSTEE

You are here now. Holds and manages assets. Has fiduciary duties. Carries obligations. Does the work. Receives no benefit.

⚠️ You service debt. You pay taxes. You comply. Others profit.

🟢 BENEFICIARY

Where you want to be. Receives the benefit. No day-to-day obligations. No management duties. Just collects.

✅ Investors. Trust beneficiaries. Silent partners.

🟡 EXECUTIVE

The CEO position. Directs. Administers. Makes decisions. Does not react. Uses the system's tools.

⚡ Business owners. Trustees of their own trusts. Holding company managers.

You were defaulted into the TRUSTEE position at birth. The other positions are available — but you must build the structures to occupy them.
