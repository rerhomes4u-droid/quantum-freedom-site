The Metabolism Expert

Who Frank Suárez was

Frank Suárez was a Puerto Rican nutritionist, author, and entrepreneur who dedicated his life to helping people understand and restore their metabolism. His book "El Poder del Metabolismo" (The Power of Your Metabolism) sold over 500,000 copies and became a best-seller across Latin America [citation:5][citation:10]. He founded NaturalSlim, a program that helped thousands of people lose weight naturally, and his YouTube channel MetabolismoTV reached millions of viewers with his educational videos [citation:14].

Born in Puerto Rico in 1950, Suárez struggled with obesity and bullying as a child, which motivated him to study metabolism and nutrition [citation:5][citation:7]. He earned a master's degree in Clinical Nutrition from the University of Puerto Rico and spent decades researching the causes of slow metabolism, obesity, and chronic disease [citation:3]. His teachings emphasized cellular nutrition, the importance of hydration, the dangers of food intolerances, and the role of the thyroid and adrenal systems in weight regulation.

⚠️ The Circumstances

Frank Suárez died on February 25, 2021, at the age of 71, after falling from the ninth-floor balcony of his apartment at Parque de Loyola in Hato Rey, San Juan, Puerto Rico [citation:4][citation:9]. Puerto Rican police ruled his death a suicide, stating that Suárez had been suffering from severe depression and had been taking antidepressants before his death [citation:4][citation:9]. His family confirmed he had been struggling with depression and had isolated himself in the months before his death [citation:6][citation:11]. However, many of his followers questioned this conclusion, noting that Suárez had frequently criticized the pharmaceutical and medical industries in his videos [citation:9][citation:11].

His Life's Mission

Teaching the world about metabolism

📖 "El Poder del Metabolismo"

Published in 2009, this book became a best-seller across Latin America, with over 500,000 copies sold [citation:5]. It documented techniques for restoring metabolism based on experience and observations of more than 25,000 people [citation:5][citation:7]. The book won the Best Health Book award at the International Latino Literary Awards in 2010 [citation:14].

Bestseller

📹 MetabolismoTV

Suárez created a YouTube channel called MetabolismoTV with over 1,600 educational videos that received more than 10 million views monthly [citation:9][citation:11]. His segment "Pregúntale a Frank" (Ask Frank) answered common questions about metabolism, thyroid, energy, digestion, and health [citation:12].

Educational Outreach

🏢 NaturalSlim

In 1998, Suárez founded NaturalSlim, a company dedicated to helping people overcome slow metabolism and obesity [citation:14]. Through this program, he helped thousands of people lose weight naturally without hunger.

Business

🧪 Cellular Nutrition

Suárez advocated for cellular nutrition, emphasizing that the body needs specific nutrients to function optimally. He taught that many chronic diseases could be prevented through proper nutrition and lifestyle changes [citation:3].

Core Teaching

⚠️ Carrageenan Warning

One of Suárez's most notable warnings was about carrageenan, a common food additive. He claimed it caused inflammation and potentially cancer, citing studies from the University of Illinois [citation:3]. This was part of his broader mission to expose harmful ingredients in processed foods.

Food Safety

💊 Critic of Pharmaceuticals

Suárez frequently criticized the pharmaceutical and medical industries, arguing that they prioritized profits over health. He believed that many medications made weight gain worse and that natural approaches were often more effective [citation:11].

The Controversy

Suicide or assassination?

🚨 The Official Ruling: Suicide

Puerto Rican police ruled Frank Suárez's death a suicide. Lieutenant Ángel Martínez, director of the Homicide Division of San Juan, confirmed that Suárez had been depressed for a long time and was taking antidepressants before his death [citation:4][citation:9]. His wife told investigators that he had been in treatment for depression for about a year and had stopped taking his medication a few months before his death [citation:9]. The family later confirmed that Suárez had been struggling with severe depression and had isolated himself [citation:6][citation:11].

💔 The Family's Confirmation

Suárez's eldest son later wrote a book about his father, confirming that "no, he was not pushed, nor was he assassinated" [citation:6]. He explained that his father suffered from severe depression that led to his tragic end. He also revealed a painful memory of finding his father with a gun when he was a child [citation:6].

Family Testimony

🤔 The Conspiracy Theories

Many of Suárez's followers refused to believe he committed suicide. They noted that he had frequently criticized the pharmaceutical and medical industries, and they speculated that he was murdered to silence him [citation:9][citation:11]. Some viral videos falsely claimed he was "assassinated by the pharmaceutical lobby" [citation:4].

Conspiracy Theory

📹 The YouTube Legacy

After his death, Suárez's health videos continued to circulate widely on social media, especially on WhatsApp. This renewed interest also fueled speculation about his death [citation:15]. His followers continue to share his teachings and question the official narrative.

Ongoing Impact

📰 Fact-Checkers Weigh In

Univision and other fact-checking organizations investigated the viral claims and concluded that Suárez was not assassinated [citation:4]. They confirmed that the police ruled his death a suicide and that his family had confirmed his struggles with depression. However, the theories continue to circulate among his followers.
